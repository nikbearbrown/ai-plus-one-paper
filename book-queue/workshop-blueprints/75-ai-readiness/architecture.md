# Learning architecture

1. Cite evidence or mark it missing for each of the six readiness questions: rules and disclosure, first attempt, disciplinary AI work, independent verification, recorded and assessed process, and evidence of learning.
2. Protect one unaided step. Delegate one narrow task to AI, or read the supplied mock output where live access is unavailable. Audit it before checking against an independent source or calculation.
3. Rewrite the assignment, write a lightweight scoring rubric and ask the student to defend a decision on a new case. Ask a peer or use the self-check to distinguish evidence from intention.
4. Decide whether to accept, revise or reject one recommendation. Keep one next step with an owner and a way to verify that it worked.

**Assessment:** pass if a peer can identify what the learner must do alone, what AI may do, what is checked independently, and what evidence shows transfer. Score reasoning and verification separately from the polish of the submission.
