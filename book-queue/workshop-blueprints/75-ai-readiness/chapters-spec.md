# Activity specifications

## 1. Diagnose an existing task

- **Input:** one offline/synthetic assignment: “Use AI to decide whether a project that costs 100 today and pays 110 in one year is worth doing at a 10% discount rate. Submit the recommendation.”
- **Activity:** identify evidence for rules/disclosure, an independent first attempt, a bounded task in the discipline, independent verification, an assessable work record, and transfer. Mark absent or unclear signals rather than inventing a policy.
- **Output:** six evidence marks and one prioritized change. Use the [framework](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/ai-readiness/blob/main/docs/framework.md) to explain the gaps.

## 2. Redesign the task and audit a plausible answer

- **Fixture:** “The net present value is 10 because 110 − 100 = 10; therefore the project clears the required return.” This is deliberately wrong: it ignores discounting.
- **Activity:** write the unaided prediction first, then read the fixture or ask AI for one critique. Name what looks wrong and why before checking with a calculator: 110/1.10 − 100 = 0. Ask for a short disclosure and decision that distinguishes break-even from positive NPV. No live AI is required.
- **Output:** a before-and-after task, with permitted AI use, independent evidence and a short process record. In another discipline, substitute one checkable claim and the corresponding independent evidence.

## 3. Score learning and make an instructor decision

- **Transfer fixture:** replacing the 110 receipt with 105 at the same rate gives an NPV of about −4.55. A student should explain the effect unaided, not just enter the number. Use a different scenario for an actual assessment.
- **Review:** award separate credit for the independent setup, evidence-backed correction, accurate disclosure and unaided explanation. Review one recommendation and record accept, revise or reject plus a reason. Optional skill path: inspect the proposed audit and suggestion artifact, not just the automatic signals.
- **Output:** a portable assessment note and a next step. Nothing is imported or published.
