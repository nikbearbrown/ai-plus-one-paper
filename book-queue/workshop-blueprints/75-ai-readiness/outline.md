# 60-minute workshop sequence

1. **0–15 minutes: Diagnose.** Mark evidence and gaps for all six readiness questions.
2. **15–40 minutes: Redesign.** Protect an initial attempt, bound AI assistance, inspect and independently check a planted flaw, then rewrite the activity.
3. **40–60 minutes: Assess and decide.** Add a short transfer question and rubric; exchange a review or do the self-check; record one implementation decision and one remaining uncertainty.

The manual activity is the default. Demonstrate the separate skill against a scrubbed fixture only if local setup is already available.
