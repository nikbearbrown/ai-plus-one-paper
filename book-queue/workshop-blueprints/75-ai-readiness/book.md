# AI Readiness: workshop brief

**Format:** one 60-minute facilitated or self-study workshop for faculty, instructors and instructional designers. No coding prerequisite. Work from one permissioned, scrubbed assignment or the synthetic exercise in [chapter specifications](chapters-spec.md).

**Core question:** What should the student be able to do independently, what can AI help with, and what evidence would convince you that the student learned?

**Takeaway:** a before-and-after task, a six-question evidence audit, a bounded AI use and disclosure rule, an independent verification step, a short process record and a transfer check. End by recording one suggested change to accept, revise or reject, with a reason. The [AI Readiness skill](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/ai-readiness) is an optional way to audit and draft suggestions, not the workshop or the decision-maker.
