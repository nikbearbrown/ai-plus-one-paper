# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Translate informal conditions into precise quantified claims
- Construct direct, contradiction and inductive proofs
- Count finite outcomes without double counting
- Represent and reason about relations and graph invariants

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish precise statements and proof habits. Build counting methods and finite probability, then use induction and graph structure to solve small reasoning problems. Final artifact: a short proof portfolio with one enumeration script independently checked by hand. Order probability immediately after the counting unit when teaching from this plan.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Require no-AI reasoning and complete proof before optional critique; then use independent counterexample search or hand enumeration to check, never as a replacement for proof. Grade semantics, proof steps, counting coverage and novel transfer.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
