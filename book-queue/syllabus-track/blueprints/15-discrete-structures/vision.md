## Editorial proposal

Discrete structures give programmers a shared language for proving statements about finite systems before they try to optimize or automate them.

**Reader and prerequisites:** Early CS learners comfortable with functions, simple algebra and tracing code; no proof-based course assumed.

**Comparable scope:** Keep Discrete Structures distinct from Algorithms: the former teaches mathematical representations and proof habits, while the latter uses those tools to design, analyze and evaluate algorithms.
