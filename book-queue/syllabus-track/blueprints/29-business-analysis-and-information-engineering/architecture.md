# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Elicit and prioritize conflicting stakeholder needs
- Build an as-is and proposed process model
- Write testable requirements with acceptance evidence
- Defend a solution and adoption plan using alternatives

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish problem and stakeholder boundaries; build process, data and requirement models; apply in a decision-ready requirements and change dossier.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade an independent stakeholder/requirements draft first, then workflow traces, acceptance checks and an alternatives defense. Verify that AI summaries do not invent requests or erase dissent.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
