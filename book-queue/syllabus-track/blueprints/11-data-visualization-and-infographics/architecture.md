# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Translate a reader question into a suitable encoding
- Reconcile chart values to auditable data
- Critique distortion and accessibility with a counterexample
- Deliver a small visual explanation checked with an independent reader task

## Sequencing and final artifact

Assimilate existing visualization and infographic candidates: establish evidence and visual encoding, build labeling and meaningful comparisons, apply narrative and accessibility review. Use optional D3/Figma tracks only for implementation.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independently sketch two alternatives and compute displayed numbers; optional AI proposes chart or copy variations; verify labels, denominators, nonvisual account and question-answer accuracy. Grade evidence correspondence, design rationale and readable comparison.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
