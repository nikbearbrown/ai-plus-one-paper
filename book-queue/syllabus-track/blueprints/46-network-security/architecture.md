# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `hold-source`. Evidence: `tentative-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Label assets and trust boundaries
- Explain why one proposed access rule is unnecessarily broad
- Triage fixed synthetic telemetry without treating correlations as proof
- Justify containment and recovery priorities in a tabletop.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish the assets and normal communication; build preventive controls and interpret defensive evidence; apply them to a tabletop with uncertainty and a recovery checklist.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade defense diagram, rule-matrix precision, artifact citations, and remediation rationale. Students draft the matrix and hypotheses independently, use AI to propose review questions, then test its interpretations against fixed synthetic evidence only.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
