# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `compare-scope`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Choose a method
- predeclare a sampling limit
- code synthetic observations
- justify a design change

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish with define an answerable question, build through the middle worked cases, then apply in make a warranted design change. Final artifact: two alternate sign placements with a documented decision and check.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independent first attempt on the opening exercise: Rewrite a broad preference question as a test. Then optionally let AI propose one change; compare it against the independent trace or calculation. Grade evidence quality, correctness on a changed case, and explanation; final demonstration: Propose a revision and falsifying observation.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
