# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `compare-scope`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. Define an answerable question

- **Observable capability:** Match a research claim to a unit of observation
- **Teaching blocks:** question; construct; contrast; bias
- **Original worked example to write:** Does a mock event poster make time easy to find?
- **Assessable practice:** Rewrite a broad preference question as a test

## 2. Appraise a study design

- **Observable capability:** Identify a method boundary before borrowing a conclusion
- **Teaching blocks:** paper reading; claim type; evidence; transfer
- **Original worked example to write:** An invented visitor study reports only volunteers
- **Assessable practice:** Identify a nontransferable conclusion and rewrite it

## 3. Choose a method

- **Observable capability:** Justify interview, observation or artifact analysis
- **Teaching blocks:** sampling; task script; ethics; scope
- **Original worked example to write:** A fictional exhibit signage walkthrough
- **Assessable practice:** Compare two methods with explicit tradeoffs

## 4. Interpret without inventing evidence

- **Observable capability:** Code observations and record disagreement
- **Teaching blocks:** coding; negative case; reflexivity; AI limits
- **Original worked example to write:** Three fabricated wayfinding observation cards
- **Assessable practice:** Produce and defend a coding memo

## 5. Make a warranted design change

- **Observable capability:** Separate findings from recommendations
- **Teaching blocks:** limitations; provisional recommendation; triangulation; reporting
- **Original worked example to write:** Two alternate sign placements
- **Assessable practice:** Propose a revision and falsifying observation
