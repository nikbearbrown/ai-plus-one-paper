# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `hold-source`. Evidence: `tentative-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Identify desired and observed-state differences
- Explain idempotence and drift using supplied snapshots
- Design change validation and rollback gates
- Produce a reproducible change proposal with readable evidence.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish meaningful configuration differences, then build an idempotent offline plan and a regression fixture, then apply the full review and rollback process to a capstone change packet.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Judge predictions before a proposed AI diff, audit only the hypothetical change plan, and verify boundaries against synthetic tests. Grade invariant preservation, safe rollout, rollback rehearsal, and documented verification.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
