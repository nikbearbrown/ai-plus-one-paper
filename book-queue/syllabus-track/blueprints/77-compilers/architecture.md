# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Parse a small language
- justify a type error
- compare interpreter and compiled output

## Sequencing and final artifact

Proposed: establish vocabulary and constraints, build a controlled evidence case, then apply it to toy expression-language compiler with equivalence checks.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

First complete the manual chapter exercise and draft the toy expression-language compiler with equivalence checks. Then, if permitted, ask AI to challenge one claim, verify with an independent trace, test or stated calculation, and record any change. Grade the artifact, verification trail and an unaided transfer case separately.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
