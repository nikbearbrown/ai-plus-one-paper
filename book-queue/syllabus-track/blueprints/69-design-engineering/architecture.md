# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Frame a contrast
- specify reusable components
- maintain responsibility boundaries
- defend integrated evidence

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish with frame the experience, build through the middle worked cases, then apply in defend the experience. Final artifact: a test person abandons a confusing step in a fictional case with a documented decision and check.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independent first attempt on the opening exercise: Build and compare two journey hypotheses. Then optionally let AI propose one change; compare it against the independent trace or calculation. Grade evidence quality, correctness on a changed case, and explanation; final demonstration: Build an eval dossier with limits and design changes.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
