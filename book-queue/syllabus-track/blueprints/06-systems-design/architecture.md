# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Turn requirements into workload and safety assumptions
- Estimate storage, throughput and latency with units
- Select semantics for data ownership and failure recovery
- Defend and revise a design using contrasting failure traces

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish requirement and sizing questions; build data semantics and failure-aware interfaces; apply an original case from design memo to tabletop incident and revised architecture.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Unaided workload sizing and network sketch; optional AI critique of one tradeoff after the initial memo; independently simulate the failure and verify revised invariants. Grade explicit assumptions, correct math, semantics and recovery proof.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
