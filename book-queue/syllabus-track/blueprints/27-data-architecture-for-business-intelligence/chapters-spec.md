# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. Start from the Decision

- **Observable capability:** Translate business questions into metric contracts.
- **Teaching blocks:** decision scenarios; grain; dimensions; quality rules
- **Original worked example to write:** A public library compares attendance across branches.
- **Assessable practice:** Identify what one event means and calculate expected attendance from fixtures.

## 2. Profile and Integrate Diverse Sources

- **Observable capability:** Reconcile differently shaped inputs without inventing values.
- **Teaching blocks:** profiling; structured and semi-structured sources; integration; validation
- **Original worked example to write:** A CSV registration list must match JSON session events.
- **Assessable practice:** Write conformance checks for changed keys and missing values.

## 3. Design Facts, Dimensions and History

- **Observable capability:** Model metrics that remain meaningful over time.
- **Teaching blocks:** star schema; keys; slowly changing dimensions; late records
- **Original worked example to write:** A library branch moves to a new district midyear.
- **Assessable practice:** Show results before and after the change with explicit accounting choices.

## 4. Publish Governed Metrics

- **Observable capability:** Expose consistent numbers with documented lineage.
- **Teaching blocks:** semantic layer; reconcile; access; metric definitions
- **Original worked example to write:** Two teams disagree over attendance because of cancellations.
- **Assessable practice:** Define both legitimate measures and validate them at the same grain.

## 5. Defend Cloud and Hybrid Choices

- **Observable capability:** Explain architecture through cost, integrity and reuse constraints.
- **Teaching blocks:** storage boundaries; workload; governance; verification packet
- **Original worked example to write:** A partner needs a de-identified extract without operational records.
- **Assessable practice:** Compare two architectures and prove one satisfies stated access and freshness requirements.
