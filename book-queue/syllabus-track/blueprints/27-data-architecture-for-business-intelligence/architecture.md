# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Specify business metrics and their grain
- Design an analytical model with justified history handling
- Integrate and validate two input shapes
- Deliver defensible, reproducible BI outputs with lineage

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish requirements and profiling; build integration and dimensional models; apply to a governed semantic model and dashboard that survive change.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade a reference calculation and intended grain individually first, then pipeline tests, dashboard-to-source reconciliation and a review note distinguishing AI suggestions from verified evidence.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
