# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Negotiate a testable brief.
- split work into owned milestones.
- validate findings with a stakeholder scenario.
- hand over a traceable artifact and personal contribution log.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish purpose and scope, build through feedback cycles, apply in a review-ready prototype and operations handoff.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Make an independent design and team contribution record before AI-assisted review. Grade traceability, individual demonstrated work, scope judgment, stakeholder validation and handoff usability.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
