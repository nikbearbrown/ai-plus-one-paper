**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Fit a small sampling baseline and evaluate its coverage
- Explain model-family tradeoffs using training and generation steps
- Compare diverse and collapsed production against a fixed test
- Design a reproducible evaluation with quality, coverage and resource limits

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish density and sampling baselines; build autoregressive, latent and adversarial generator comparisons; apply controlled evaluation across modalities. Iterative denoising is an optional editorial extension, not observed scope.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Before help, sketch each family’s sampling and count failure patterns in toy data; optional AI predicts pitfalls, then test with fixed seeds, held-out points and counterexamples. Grade derivation, evaluation design and reproducible tradeoffs.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
