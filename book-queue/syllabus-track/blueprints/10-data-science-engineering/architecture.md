# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Write a testable question and record dataset provenance
- Diagnose missingness, schema changes and leakage
- Build interpretable baselines and valid held-out evaluations
- Communicate uncertainty and practical limits using reproducible artifacts

## Sequencing and final artifact

Assimilate existing Data Science Engineering entry: establish empirical question and prepared records, build baseline/model and held-out comparisons, apply subgroup/error interpretation and decision rationale.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Do no-AI exploratory chart and split plan first; optional AI proposes alternative features only after baselines. Replay on an unseen synthetic slice; grade provenance, valid split, metric choice and limitations.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
