# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. Failure scenarios and consequences

- **Observable capability:** Turn an operational dependency into an explicit risk pathway.
- **Teaching blocks:** Asset and impact; fault-tree logic; dependencies; failure modes and effects.
- **Original worked example to write:** Map missed food-delivery trips caused by one scheduler outage.
- **Assessable practice:** Build a fault tree and failure-modes-and-effects table for four scenarios, then justify their priority.

## 2. Risk quantification without false precision

- **Observable capability:** Compare scenarios under changed assumptions.
- **Teaching blocks:** Likelihood bands; downtime cost; sensitivity; uncertainty.
- **Original worked example to write:** Estimate outage cost at three service volumes and two restoration speeds.
- **Assessable practice:** Show where two mitigations swap rank as uncertainty changes.

## 3. Controls and continuity

- **Observable capability:** Propose controls that can be tested before a disruption.
- **Teaching blocks:** Prevention; detection; recovery time; recovery point.
- **Original worked example to write:** Compare manual fallbacks and a read-only mirror for a student service.
- **Assessable practice:** Specify measurable pass criteria for a recovery drill.

## 4. From incidents to evidence

- **Observable capability:** Write a risk decision and verify whether controls operated.
- **Teaching blocks:** Tabletop roles; leading indicators; incident timelines; remediation.
- **Original worked example to write:** Review a synthetic duplicate shipment and determine which check failed.
- **Assessable practice:** Produce a risk memo with test evidence, owner and follow-through date.
