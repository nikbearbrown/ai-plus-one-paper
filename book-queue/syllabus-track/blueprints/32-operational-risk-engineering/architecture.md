# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Create a scenario risk register.
- compare preventive and detective controls.
- estimate uncertainty in a loss or downtime model.
- evaluate an incident exercise.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish failure and exposure; quantify and compare safeguards; apply with a tabletop exercise and a residual-risk recommendation.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

First write independent failure hypotheses, then audit assistant suggestions against synthetic incident evidence. Grade causal clarity, uncertainty, prioritization, and tested control ownership.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
