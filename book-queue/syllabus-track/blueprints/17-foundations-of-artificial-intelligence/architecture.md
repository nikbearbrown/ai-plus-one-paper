# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Represent a decision problem as states, actions and objective
- Compare two reasoning choices by reproducible metrics
- Explain why a model result changes when its assumptions change

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish representation; build deterministic and uncertainty-aware reasoning; apply them in a comparative decision lab.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade independent problem model and traced decisions, then a test set and interpretation; students independently derive expected outputs before comparing AI feedback.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
