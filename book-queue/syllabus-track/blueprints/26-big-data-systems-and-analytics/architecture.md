# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `hold-source`. Evidence: `tentative-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Choose an analytics data path with explicit assumptions
- Trace the provenance of a reported decision
- Evaluate quality, latency and cost on the same benchmark

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish structured inputs and a decision goal; build a bounded analytic data pipeline; apply an evaluation protocol in a synthetic case.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade independent expected outputs, lineage and failure predictions before AI feedback, then benchmark evidence and a decision log with uncaught cases.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
