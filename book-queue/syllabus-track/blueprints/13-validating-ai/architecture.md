# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Extract testable claims and governing scope from fluent output
- Design a check independent of the generator and detect weak evidence
- Use uncertainty, bias or robustness tests to calibrate a decision
- Prepare auditable results and apply a defensible stop or handoff rule

## Sequencing and final artifact

Assimilate the Computational Skepticism manuscript/course: establish claims and uncertainty, build verification/robustness/fairness checks, apply to an agentic evaluation and public-facing decision note.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Require an unaided claim, prediction and test list; preserve a short revision reflection. Optional AI gives challenge cases, not verdict. Grade source independence, test validity, qualified conclusions and final unaided transfer; do not conflate reflective records with validated performance gains.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
