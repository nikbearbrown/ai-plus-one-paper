# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `hold-source`. Evidence: `tentative-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Relate use-case requirements to radio and core functions
- Distinguish control from user plane events on a diagram
- Compare service architectures against a supplied budget
- Validate a design recommendation with a simulated case.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish three service profiles before naming technologies, build radio and core models separately, then combine them in a service architecture assessment with a mobility and capacity test.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade a human-drawn service path and budget, a justified design split, and retained uncertainties. AI may propose alternatives only after baseline calculations; check claims against instructor-provided standard excerpts before acceptance.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
