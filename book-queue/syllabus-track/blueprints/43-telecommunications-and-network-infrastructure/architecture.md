# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Calculate a link-budget margin.
- map transport through optical/radio/IP layers.
- interpret latency, capacity and availability budgets.
- defend a resilient design against failure simulations.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish layer interfaces and unit discipline; build link/service models; apply to a tabletop design across a synthetic regional communications network.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Compute a manual baseline before using AI to compare topologies; grade power and unit checks, defensible modeling assumptions, redundancy evidence, and explicit service tradeoffs.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
