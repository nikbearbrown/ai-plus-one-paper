# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Prove a transformation behaves consistently on given inputs
- Construct a typed transformation pipeline
- Diagnose shuffle and skew with observed metrics
- Reconcile local and distributed outputs

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish purity and types; build Spark transformations, grouping and joins; apply them in a partition-sensitive batch pipeline with checks.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade hand-computed expected records and invariants, implementation and performance evidence; AI may propose a refactor only after baseline outputs exist and must survive comparison.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
