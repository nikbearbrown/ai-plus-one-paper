# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Read identity and permission information
- Connect a local process to a service observation
- Classify an interface, routing, or naming symptom using fixtures
- Produce a reproducible read-only service handoff.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish shell, identity, and filesystem vocabulary; build host-to-network observation skills; apply them to a handoff that labels verified findings, hypotheses, and proposed safe changes.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Require a no-AI first hypothesis for a synthetic service failure, a precise output line citation, and a minimal safe next check. Compare AI’s diagnosis against the fixture and grade corrections as well as the final handoff.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
