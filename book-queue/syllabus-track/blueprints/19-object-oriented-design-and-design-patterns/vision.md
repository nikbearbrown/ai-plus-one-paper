## Editorial proposal

Design tested objects around stable responsibilities, then introduce patterns only when a concrete change makes the simpler design inadequate.

**Reader and prerequisites:** Graduate or advanced undergraduate programmer who has implemented small programs but has not designed larger collaborations.

**Comparable scope:** One progressive volume for the overlapping OO/patterns courses, with an independently adoptable upper part. Keep the product, persistence and delivery scope of Application Engineering separate; compare existing Java books for whether to use this as a design supplement.
