# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `compare-scope`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Formulate an observable research question
- Translate evidence into alternative flows
- Design and run a bounded usability protocol
- Revise and defend an experience using traceable evidence.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish observation and task framing, build alternative flows and low-fidelity prototypes, then apply a think-aloud test and revision in a case-study dossier. An optional advanced module adds competitive scope and success metrics.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Use synthetic or consented minimal user data only. Grade an original flow and unaided observation ledger, then use AI to suggest alternative interpretations and check those against observed evidence; require revision rationale and explicit sample limits.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
