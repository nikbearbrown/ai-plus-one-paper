# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Compare project options with explicit selection criteria
- Explain traditional and iterative delivery choices
- Build a dependency and risk register
- Defend a portfolio recommendation with verifiable decision evidence.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish the business need and criteria, build a plan with costs, dependencies and gates, then apply oversight to a four-stage case dossier and changed constraint.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade decision traceability, coherent cost/risk assumptions, dependencies and stakeholder authority. Start with a hand-scored option matrix; use AI to red-team the rationale and validate every suggested tradeoff against the case.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
