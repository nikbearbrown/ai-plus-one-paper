# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `compare-scope`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Predict image position
- compare tolerance tradeoffs
- design a bench test
- reconcile model and data

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish with set the optical requirement, build through the middle worked cases, then apply in plan the bench test. Final artifact: compare centered and shifted target images with a documented decision and check.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independent first attempt on the opening exercise: State acceptable image size and distance. Then optionally let AI propose one change; compare it against the independent trace or calculation. Grade evidence quality, correctness on a changed case, and explanation; final demonstration: Draft a measurement sheet and rejection condition.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
