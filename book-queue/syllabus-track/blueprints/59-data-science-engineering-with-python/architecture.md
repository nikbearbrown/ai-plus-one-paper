## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independent first attempt on the opening exercise: Design a check for a changing station ID. Then optionally let AI propose one change; compare it against the independent trace or calculation. Grade evidence quality, correctness on a changed case, and explanation; final demonstration: Provide a rerun note and failing input test.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
