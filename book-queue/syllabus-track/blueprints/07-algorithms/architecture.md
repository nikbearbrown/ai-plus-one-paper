# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Design a graph or sequence representation around input/output promises
- Prove correctness using invariants, exchange or induction
- Derive time and space bounds and test their assumptions experimentally
- Choose between an algorithmic paradigm and a baseline from a counterexample
- Classify a reduction’s direction and explain what it establishes

## Sequencing and final artifact

Assimilate into Algorithms AI Plus One: establish contracts, growth and data structures; build search, branching, greedy, decomposition and dynamic programming; apply with reductions, robustness and reproducible analysis. Common kernel covers CS3000 abstraction/proof and INFO6205 engineering; track distinctions belong in optional labs, not rival books.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Separate math evidence (proof, trace, asymptotic derivation) from execution evidence (tests, scaling observations, environment). Attempt path/cost proof unaided before optional AI critique; require unseen transfer and explain one contradictory model suggestion.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
