# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Trace a business risk to controls and owners.
- distinguish design from operating effectiveness.
- compare compliance evidence against a stated requirement.
- document an AI-use exception with independent verification.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish governance boundaries; build risk and control maps; apply through a defensive audit simulation and a remediation plan.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Make control judgments from synthetic evidence first; verify AI summaries independently. Grade traceability, scope, evidence sufficiency, proportional remediation and privacy protection.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
