# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. Governance boundaries and assets

- **Observable capability:** Identify who owns decisions and which systems are in scope.
- **Teaching blocks:** Asset inventories; accountable roles; data classification; scope statements.
- **Original worked example to write:** Draw a synthetic small nonprofit's SaaS system and identify owners.
- **Assessable practice:** Mark in-scope evidence and justify two boundary exclusions.

## 2. Risk to control mapping

- **Observable capability:** Map risks to directly testable preventive and detective controls.
- **Teaching blocks:** Risk statements; control objectives; compensating controls; responsibility.
- **Original worked example to write:** Compare shared inbox approvals with named reviewer approvals in a simulated workflow.
- **Assessable practice:** Produce a control matrix with evidence, owner and validation method.

## 3. Evidence for audit

- **Observable capability:** Distinguish a policy promise from an operating control.
- **Teaching blocks:** Sampling; evidence strength; exceptions; audit trail.
- **Original worked example to write:** Assess a support team access review with four fabricated monthly snapshots.
- **Assessable practice:** Rate each finding and propose a follow-up that resolves uncertainty.

## 4. Governance for AI systems

- **Observable capability:** Construct an approval and monitoring process for AI-enabled work.
- **Teaching blocks:** Use-case inventory; human oversight; vendor evidence; remediation.
- **Original worked example to write:** Review a hypothetical model-generated response assistant proposed by finance operations.
- **Assessable practice:** Write an exception memo with restricted data boundaries and a reassessment date.
