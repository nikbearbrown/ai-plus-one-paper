## Editorial proposal

Build a verified toolkit of arithmetic proofs in Z, transfer it to modular and polynomial settings, and use it to explain what changes in approximation, quadratic rings and analytic number theory.

**Reader and prerequisites:** Two independently adoptable parts: Part I for undergraduates with algebra and introductory discrete proofs; Part II for readers who pass a short congruence, gcd and Euclidean-domain diagnostic, with an abstract-algebra/convergence bridge before advanced work.

**Comparable scope:** Merge MATH3527 and MATH4527 as a single Number Theory book with two independently adoptable parts. Shared foundation is divisibility, congruence, units, Euclidean division, and what factorization requires. Split only if independent adoption or length forces separate physical volumes; preserve the shared notation and bridge either way. Keep MATH7359 Elliptic Curves and Modular Forms separate: professional depth in algebraic geometry, finite/complex fields and modular forms is not delivered by the Number Theory sequence.
