- Map user and model responsibility
- design refusal/recovery

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish with set expectations, build through the middle worked cases, then apply in test mental models. Final artifact: mock test with fictional participant responses with a documented decision and check.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independent first attempt on the opening exercise: Draft an interface warning with a user action. Then optionally let AI propose one change; compare it against the independent trace or calculation. Grade evidence quality, correctness on a changed case, and explanation; final demonstration: Explain what the observations can and cannot support.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
