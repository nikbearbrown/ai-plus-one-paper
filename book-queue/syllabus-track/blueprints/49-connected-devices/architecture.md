# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Trace a sensor message through the full system
- Compare messaging designs under interruption
- Specify device onboarding and lifecycle expectations
- Demonstrate a recovery and data-quality contract in simulation.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish the measurement journey, build a message and identity contract, then apply the design to offline and reconnect scenarios for a final system dossier.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade message trace, explicit behavior for duplicate or missing events, privacy constraints and failure recovery. Draft a prediction individually; use AI to nominate edge cases, then test them against the deterministic fixtures.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
