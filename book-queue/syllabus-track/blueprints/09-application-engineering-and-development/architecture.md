- Model actors and operations with domain invariants
- Separate interaction, domain and persistence responsibilities
- Validate complete user-flow transactions including error paths
- Use tests and refactoring to justify a feature change

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Hand-model domain and failure cases unaided, then implement; optional AI proposes a refactor or test, independently verify regression. Grade fit with requirements, invariant checks, usable flow and quality of tests over framework count.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
