# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `reuse-private-pilot`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Write a competency question with an expected answer and unknown rule
- Encode and query one evidence set as property graph and RDF
- Separate asserted, entailed and invalid-for-contract statements
- Audit a merge decision and choose local or collection-level retrieval
- Defend an architecture against baseline, error and abstention cases

## Sequencing and final artifact

Assimilate the existing Knowledge Graphs pilot: its seven chapters move from representation and paired queries through meaning/validation to construction, retrieval and defense on one synthetic library fixture.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Reuse the pilot’s trace, validation record, merge ledger, claim-evidence matrix and oral defense. First predict answers without AI; then check optional query suggestions against fixtures, deliberate missing evidence and baseline differences. Grade by correct results, source traceability and error diagnosis; independent transfer case before reveal.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
