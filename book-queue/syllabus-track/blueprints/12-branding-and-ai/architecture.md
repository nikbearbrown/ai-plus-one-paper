# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Derive a testable positioning statement from defined audiences and constraints
- Translate principles into copy/style/tokens and coherent deliverables
- Compare options using voice, accessibility and reputational criteria
- Document bounded agent work, human decisions and independent quality tests

## Sequencing and final artifact

Assimilate into existing Branding and AI: establish identity and audience, build consistent system and variants, apply to a campaign with oversight and change requests. Term-specific briefs stay out.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Learner writes original brief and creates initial example without AI; later generates variations within explicit exclusions and checks them with token, reading and consent rules. Grade strategy, alignment, process rationale and independent defense.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
