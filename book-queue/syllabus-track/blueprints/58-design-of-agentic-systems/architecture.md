**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `existing-book`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Separate deterministic and model steps
- specify tool and stop contracts
- compare a baseline on failure cases

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish with frame the decision, build through the middle worked cases, then apply in check the trace. Final artifact: a completed transcript with no reservation receipt with a documented decision and check.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Independent first attempt on the opening exercise: Locate two choices that should not be delegated. Then optionally let AI propose one change; compare it against the independent trace or calculation. Grade evidence quality, correctness on a changed case, and explanation; final demonstration: Specify the trace fields and acceptance tests.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
