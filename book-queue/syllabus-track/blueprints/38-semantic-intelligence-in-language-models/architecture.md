# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Train and inspect a miniature language model.
- compare adaptation to prompting under fixed evidence.
- retrieve linked text, table and figure context.
- audit factual support and abstentions.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish token prediction and model choice; build adaptation and context reconstruction; apply to a cited technical-document assistant evaluated against a flat retrieval baseline.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Produce a human evidence map before AI assistance; grade held-out grounded accuracy, unsupported-claim detection, ranking ablation and cost reporting with replayable cases.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
