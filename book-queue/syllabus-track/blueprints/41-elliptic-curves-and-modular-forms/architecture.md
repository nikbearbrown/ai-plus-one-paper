# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Verify nonsingular curve models and calculate the group law.
- connect divisors to genus-one geometry.
- bound finite-field counts.
- relate complex uniformization to periods.
- compare initial modular-form and curve data without claiming a finite check proves modularity.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish curve arithmetic and geometry, build finite and complex perspectives, apply to an evidence dossier comparing one curve's local traces and modular coefficients.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Prove identities manually and check with an assistant or computer algebra on small fields; grade hypotheses, correct arithmetic, valid theorem scope and explicit distinction between computation and proof.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
