# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. Cubic models and the group law

- **Observable capability:** Identify nonsingularity and compute point addition.
- **Teaching blocks:** Discriminants; Weierstrass models; tangent and chord; point at infinity.
- **Original worked example to write:** Check doubling of (0,1) on y²=x³−x+1.
- **Assessable practice:** Compute and verify two point sums and discuss the exceptional vertical-line case.

## 2. Divisors and maps on genus-one curves

- **Observable capability:** Explain geometric constraints using functions and divisors.
- **Teaching blocks:** Projective closure; divisors; differentials; isogeny degree.
- **Original worked example to write:** Compute a simple rational function's zeros and poles on a projective model.
- **Assessable practice:** Reconcile divisor degrees and explain when an affine count misses a point.

## 3. Point counts over finite fields

- **Observable capability:** Count points and compare with the Hasse bound.
- **Teaching blocks:** Quadratic characters; Frobenius trace; good reduction; finite-field sanity checks.
- **Original worked example to write:** Enumerate a model's points over F5 and F7 and compute resulting traces.
- **Assessable practice:** Produce a checked count for a third good prime and document excluded bad primes.

## 4. The complex torus perspective

- **Observable capability:** Explain how a lattice supplies the complex addition model.
- **Teaching blocks:** Periodic functions; lattices; Weierstrass function; uniformization.
- **Original worked example to write:** Compare scaling of two simple lattice diagrams and inferred periods.
- **Assessable practice:** Classify transformed basis vectors that preserve versus change the lattice.

## 5. From modular forms to curve evidence

- **Observable capability:** Interpret initial coefficients and state valid conclusions.
- **Teaching blocks:** Congruence subgroup; q-expansion; Hecke action; L-series correspondence.
- **Original worked example to write:** Compare a classroom curve's first good-prime traces to a supplied licensed coefficient table.
- **Assessable practice:** Report agreements and inconsistencies with a method that cannot mistake data matching for proof.
