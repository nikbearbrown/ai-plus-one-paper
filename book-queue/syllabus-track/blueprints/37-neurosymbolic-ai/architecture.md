# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Model system requirements and traceability as explicit facts and rules.
- Query a small knowledge graph and identify missing evidence.
- Validate an LLM-proposed change against one symbolic constraint and one solver check.
- Compare acceptance errors and explanation quality against a model-only baseline.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish requirements and typed knowledge; build query, rule and solver checks; apply in a synthetic engineering-change dossier with reviewed traces, blocked proposals and auditable acceptance criteria.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Solve rules and edge cases independently, then check AI-suggested clauses against counterexamples. Grade trace correctness, conflict diagnosis, uncertainty handling and controlled baseline comparison.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
