# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `hold-source`. Evidence: `tentative-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Draw a packet path with the correct address at each hop
- Check an address against a prefix and justify a route choice
- Separate name resolution, transport, and forwarding failures
- Defend a diagnosis using supplied synthetic traces.
- Compare two Internet service designs under policy, traffic-class and scaling constraints

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish a short addressing and forwarding refresher, build an end-to-end distinction among naming, transport and route policy, then apply it to service quality, IPv6 coexistence and a synthetic architecture dossier.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade an annotated unseen trace for address changes, route selection, alternative diagnoses, and a disconfirming observation; learner predicts by hand, uses AI to propose alternatives, and rechecks them against the supplied table.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
