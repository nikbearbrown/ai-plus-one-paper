# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Match a confidentiality, integrity, or authenticity requirement to an appropriate primitive
- Explain a toy modular arithmetic identity
- Distinguish certificate, peer and credential roles
- Identify lifecycle failures and write a defensible protection plan.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish security properties and elementary mathematical terms, build primitive and identity distinctions, apply them to a trusted-library design and key rotation tabletop.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade a closed-notes property justification, synthetic identity chain diagram, and independently checked lifecycle plan. AI may offer edge-case review only after the model has been locked; do not submit key material or use generated production cryptography.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
