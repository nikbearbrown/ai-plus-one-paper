# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Map a process with data and responsibilities.
- diagnose a throughput constraint.
- compare interventions by delay, cost and error.
- define a monitored rollout.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish the real workflow; build a discrete comparison model; apply to a redesign packet with baseline, process map and approval criteria.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Draw the baseline independently before using AI to propose improvements. Grade fidelity to event traces, explicit assumptions, measurable impact and treatment of edge cases.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
