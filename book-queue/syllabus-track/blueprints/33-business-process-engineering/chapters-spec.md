# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. Observe the service as performed

- **Observable capability:** Build an accurate current-state model from a trace.
- **Teaching blocks:** Process boundaries; swimlanes; roles; exception routing.
- **Original worked example to write:** Map a permit application that returns for missing evidence.
- **Assessable practice:** Reconstruct a process from 12 synthetic events and identify two uncertain handoffs.

## 2. Capacity, queues and waste

- **Observable capability:** Identify bottlenecks and estimate their consequences.
- **Teaching blocks:** Throughput; variability; waiting; rework.
- **Original worked example to write:** Compare one long inspection with a short precheck and new queue.
- **Assessable practice:** Calculate what a faster intake does and does not fix.

## 3. Design alternative future states

- **Observable capability:** Compare changes against agreed outcomes.
- **Teaching blocks:** Simplification; controls; automation opportunities; accessibility.
- **Original worked example to write:** Move an approval upstream without giving one person unilateral authority.
- **Assessable practice:** Produce two alternatives and score them for cost, delay, errors and inclusion.

## 4. Pilot, measure and learn

- **Observable capability:** Plan a pilot with a baseline and stop rule.
- **Teaching blocks:** Event logging; KPI definition; performance guardrails; phased rollout.
- **Original worked example to write:** Trial a document checklist with alternating cohorts.
- **Assessable practice:** Design a rollout report and specify when to roll back.
