# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `compare-scope`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Separate a theorem or definition from an empirical proxy
- Analyze how privacy and robustness claims depend on assumptions
- Compare calibration or uncertainty behavior across provided groups
- Design a research proposal with disconfirming evidence.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish the anatomy of a trustworthiness claim, build two formal and empirical perspectives, then apply them to a preregistered-style research brief with a proof sketch, synthetic experiment, and limitation statement.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Grade an independently written definition and counterexample, then comparison of AI feedback with an assigned source and synthetic fixture. Capstone grades hypothesis, identifiable failure condition, uncertainty and reflection; no empirical result fabricated.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
