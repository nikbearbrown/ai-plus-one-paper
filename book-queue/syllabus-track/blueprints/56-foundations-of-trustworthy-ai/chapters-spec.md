# Chapter specifications

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `compare-scope`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

Each entry is a proposed chapter, not drafted prose.

## 1. The claim and its assumptions

- **Observable capability:** Restate a trustworthiness claim so that it is testable
- **Teaching blocks:** definitions; quantifiers; counterexamples; evidence bounds
- **Original worked example to write:** A toy classifier is called trustworthy after one average score.
- **Assessable practice:** Write two precise alternative claims and a counterexample for one of them.

## 2. Privacy claims have a boundary

- **Observable capability:** Analyze what an abstract private computation guarantee covers
- **Teaching blocks:** data adjacency idea; outputs; assumptions; utility tradeoff
- **Original worked example to write:** A synthetic survey's two candidate summary rules expose different outcomes when a record changes.
- **Assessable practice:** Compare claims under the stated assumptions using only the supplied synthetic examples.

## 3. Generalization is not robustness

- **Observable capability:** Design a targeted stress test without changing what a baseline predicts
- **Teaching blocks:** in-distribution evidence; shifts; perturbation model; held-out testing
- **Original worked example to write:** A fixture changes label prevalence and one feature correlation across two toy groups.
- **Assessable practice:** Compute two comparable summary tables and explain which assumption failed.

## 4. When uncertainty is well explained

- **Observable capability:** Check a confidence-based policy under grouping and shift
- **Teaching blocks:** calibration; coverage; conditional behavior; abstention
- **Original worked example to write:** A mock answer model has equal aggregate calibration across two operating contexts but different coverage.
- **Assessable practice:** Calculate per-context counts and revise the abstention threshold rule against a specified cost.

## 5. Propose a trustworthiness study

- **Observable capability:** Build a defensible research test with a falsifier and limits
- **Teaching blocks:** hypothesis; formal assumptions; baselines; reproducibility
- **Original worked example to write:** An invented intake system claims a robust human-deferral policy.
- **Assessable practice:** Submit a two-page protocol and defend one assumption with a counterexample or controlled test.
