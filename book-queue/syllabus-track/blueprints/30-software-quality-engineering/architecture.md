# Learning architecture

**Private Blueprint only. Draft route: `ready-for-draft-review`. Evidence: `matched-source`. No sign-off.**

## Proposed measurable outcomes

- Translate requirements into testable risks.
- design equivalence, boundary and property cases.
- detect escaped faults with mutation tests.
- publish a reproducible release recommendation.

## Sequencing and final artifact

Establish a quality claim and risk model; build a layered suite and feedback loop; apply to a small release with a test strategy, defect log, and signed-off residual-risk memo.

## Assessment and AI Plus One practice

Author a test oracle independently, then compare an AI-generated suite. Grade failing-case discovery, false positives, coverage of risks, determinism, and explanation using a concealed mutation set.

Require a first attempt without AI; document any bounded AI assistance and independently check consequential outputs against evidence. These are original assessment recommendations, not institutional assignments or grading policy.
