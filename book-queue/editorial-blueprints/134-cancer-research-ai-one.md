## Purpose and reader

## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Forming an answerable question.** Translate a published conclusion into a bounded research question.
2. **Design, ethics and evidence hierarchy.** Compare two hypothetical observational designs and identify confounding.
3. **Observational data reasoning.** Recalculate an aggregate precision measure from synthetic data.
4. **Evidence synthesis and reproducibility.** Build a documented figure audit and distinguish verification from replication.
5. **Responsible scientific communication.** Prepare a short reproducible claim memo with explicit nonclaims.

## Assessed deliverable

Build a retrospective claim audit of openly published aggregate results with a synthetic replication exercise; grade evidence traceability, uncertainty and independent recheck.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
