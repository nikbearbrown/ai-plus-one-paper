## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Economic models and assumptions.** Explain which variables move in a toy model and falsify a misleading interpretation.
2. **Measuring markets and living standards.** Reconcile price-adjusted series and base-year changes.
3. **Identification and confounding.** Compare a back-of-envelope pattern with a synthetic controlled counterexample.
4. **Institutions and distribution.** Model how two groups can be affected differently by the same change.
5. **Decision under model uncertainty.** Write a policy comparison with sensitivity and alternative mechanisms.
