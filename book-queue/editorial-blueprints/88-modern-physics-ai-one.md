# Modern Physics (AI Plus One)

**Order 88.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Explain where classical predictions fail and how relativity and quantum reasoning account for specific observations.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Students who have completed calculus-based introductory physics; linear algebra useful but not required for the bridge.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/physics-modern-physics` as primary and `nikbearbrown/physics-plus-one-modern-physics; nikbearbrown/claude-for-physics` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Special relativity.** Output: Use spacetime relations to compare two clocks or frames. Check: Distinguish proper time from a coordinate time.
2. **Quantization and wave-particle evidence.** Output: Predict trends in a simple light–matter dataset. Check: Separate measured effects from interpretive assumptions.
3. **Elementary quantum states.** Output: Calculate probabilities and energies in a finite model. Check: Check normalization and the classical-scale contrast.
4. **Atomic, nuclear and solid-state overview.** Output: Compare how simplified models explain spectra and material behavior. Check: Identify limits of a model without making instrumental or hazardous claims.

## Assessed deliverable

A modern-physics evidence report that independently reproduces three introductory predictions and states where each model fails. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.

## Drafting gate

Compare older and current editions. Keep distinct from a full quantum mechanics or quantum computing text; independently verify diagrams and problem keys.
