## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **From cells to tumor diversity.** Build a causal diagram with labeled gaps and competing explanations.
2. **Reading the evidence.** Classify mechanistic, observational and outcome claims against independent sources.
3. **Therapeutic concepts in context.** Compare broad conceptual classes using a nonclinical evidence rubric.
4. **Measurement and translation.** Trace how a public research figure supports only a bounded inference.
5. **Integrated study map.** Develop a patient-free explanatory portfolio with review checkpoints and source receipts.
