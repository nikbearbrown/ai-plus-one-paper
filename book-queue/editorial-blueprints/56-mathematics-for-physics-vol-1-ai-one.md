# Mathematics for Physics Volume 1 (AI Plus One)

**Order 56.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Build the mathematical toolkit that turns a physical description into a solvable, checkable model.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Students with introductory calculus preparing for calculus-based physics.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/math-for-physics` as primary and `nikbearbrown/claude-for-physics` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Scales and vectors.** Output: Set up dimensional checks and vector decompositions. Check: Verify a change of units preserves the prediction.
2. **Differentiation and integration.** Output: Translate motion constraints and cumulative effects into equations. Check: Check limiting cases.
3. **Linear differential models.** Output: Solve a simple oscillator or growth model from initial conditions. Check: Check by substitution and units.
4. **Modes and approximations.** Output: Use expansions or linear combinations to simplify a model. Check: Quantify where a first-order approximation stops agreeing.

## Assessed deliverable

A mathematical modeling portfolio containing predicted limits, original derivations, computational comparisons and a short correction log. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
