# Investing in Quantum

Private workshop Blueprint • `investing-in-quantum-ai-one` • 60 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Business and technology learners with basic table reading; no trading experience.

**Case:** Two fictional quantum companies and a seller's mock technical claim.

## Run of show

- 0–10: Write independent evidence requirements for a performance claim.
- 10–25: Separate technical milestone, deployment evidence and revenue assumptions.
- 25–45: Ask AI or inspect supplied prose for a counterargument; classify each statement and look up only designated evidence cards.
- 45–60: Peer challenges one assumption and rewrites the claim as a qualified finding.

## Artifact and verification

A two-company diligence matrix and one evidence-bounded comparison; reconcile each assertion to its card.

**Boundary:** Educational fictional case only; no forecasts, recommendations or transactions. Any actual data need current primary verification and figure rights.
