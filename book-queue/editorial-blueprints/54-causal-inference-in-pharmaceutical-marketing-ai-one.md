# Causal Inference in Pharmaceutical Marketing (AI Plus One)

**Order 54.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Separate the effect of professional marketing exposure from noisy observational associations using auditable methods.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Analysts with regression, introductory probability and study-design experience.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/pharma-rep-visit-data-as-an-untapped-causal-dataset` as primary and `nikbearbrown/claude-for-pharmaceutical-marketing` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Define estimand.** Output: Write target population, time window, intervention and outcome for a synthetic case. Check: Check whether the quantity is identifiable from proposed data.
2. **Model confounding.** Output: Draw a causal graph with measured and unmeasured causes. Check: Explain why a tempting adjustment is invalid.
3. **Study design and diagnostics.** Output: Choose a comparison design and balancing checks. Check: Demonstrate one assumption failure in simulated data.
4. **Sensitivity and limits.** Output: Run a sensitivity analysis and write a no-claim condition. Check: Report which conclusion survives the plausible alternatives.

## Assessed deliverable
