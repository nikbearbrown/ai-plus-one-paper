# Music, Imitation, and AI

**Workshop · 45 min · Queue 121 · Private Blueprint · hold**

**Audience and prerequisites.** Musicians, educators or media students; no music-generation account needed.

**Timed exercise.** 0–8 read an original two-bar rhythm and mark relevant features; 8–20 compare two instructor-created transformations; 20–32 blind and swap labels for a peer comparison; 32–45 separate similarity, authorship and imitation claims in a short memo.

**Checked take-home artifact.** Feature checklist for tempo, rhythm and contour with an independent manual score and uncertainty statement. Repeat after one controlled change.

**Scope and caution.** Use self-created notation/audio only; no artist cloning, commercial audio, publishing, or authorship verdicts from similarity. Private research titles only.
