## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Bits, qubits and measurement.** Manually derive probabilities and compare to sampled distributions.
2. **Gates and circuit composition.** Build equivalent tiny circuits and compare results under basis choices.
3. **Entanglement and correlations.** Distinguish correlated outputs from a factorized state in a simulation.
4. **Small algorithmic examples.** Analyze query counts for a harmless toy oracle and check the classical comparison.
5. **Noise and implementation.** Compare ideal to noisy outcomes and justify where the result is no longer informative.

## Assessed deliverable

Deliver a circuit notebook with hand predictions and analytic checks on a noiseless and noisy simulator; grade normalization, circuit size, sampling limits and reproducibility.
