## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Ray models and image formation.** Draw rays for a thin lens and reconcile image sign with a sketch.
2. **Interfaces and total internal reflection.** Plot refraction angle; independently compute the critical angle.
3. **Aberrations and practical optics.** Compare ideal and off-axis focus diagrams; explain a measured trend.
4. **Interference and diffraction.** Predict slit-fringe spacing and check limiting cases.
5. **Polarization and resolution.** Use a synthetic intensity series to distinguish attenuation and polarization.
