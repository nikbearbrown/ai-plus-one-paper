## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Undergraduate or early MBA readers with no management experience required.

**Teaching spine:** Make accountable operational decisions under uncertainty about people, structures and constraints.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Define work worth managing.** Turn a vague goal into a measurable outcome and decision log; challenge a harmful proxy.
2. **Design roles and structure.** Draw responsibilities and dependencies; diagnose a handoff where nobody owns a decision.
3. **Understand incentives and motivation.** Compare two incentive designs; predict a measurable side effect and a mitigation.
4. **Build an operating routine.** Plan staffing and feedback cadence around a constrained queue; test capacity with an abnormal week.
5. **Lead change with feedback.** Prepare a reversible trial and feedback protocol; separate adoption evidence from interpretation.

## Assessed deliverable

Design a turnaround review for a fictional volunteer distribution team. Grade problem framing, stakeholder incentives, decision rationale, actual outcome measures and an evidence-based revision plan.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
