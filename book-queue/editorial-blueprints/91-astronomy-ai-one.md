## Purpose and reader

Infer the structure and development of astronomical systems from observations with clearly stated uncertainties.

**Audience and prerequisites:** General college readers comfortable with ratios, graphs, and basic mechanics.

## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Time, sky and motion.** Predict one seasonal sky pattern using a diagram and check against a public ephemeris.
2. **Light as evidence.** Infer a relative temperature from a toy spectrum and state degeneracies.
3. **Gravity, orbits and transits.** Estimate orbital period or radius from a public light curve with uncertainty.
4. **Stellar lives and populations.** Read an HR diagram and test a cluster-age interpretation.
5. **Galaxies and expansion.** Estimate a slope from public redshift-distance data and separate motion from model assumption.

## Assessed deliverable

Construct a public-data observation-to-inference notebook for a star or exoplanet; score units, calibration, competing explanations, and evidence trail.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
