# The Learning Engineer

**Workshop · 60 min · Queue 111 · Private Blueprint · hold**

**Audience and prerequisites.** Faculty, designers and learning technologists; basic familiarity with learning objectives.

**Timed exercise.** 0–12 state one observable outcome; 12–25 sketch a low-stakes prototype and compare baseline; 25–40 inspect synthetic learner logs for one alternative explanation; 40–60 propose one revision and an outcome measure with guardrails.

**Checked take-home artifact.** Mini evaluation protocol and decision memo. Peer checks goal-to-measure alignment, avoids counting completion as learning, and identifies one missing assumption.

**Scope and caution.** Private draft lead only; no research participants or course records. Distinguish trial design and measurement from general learning experience design.
