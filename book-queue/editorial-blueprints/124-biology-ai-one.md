## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Testable explanations in biology.** Compare supplied competing hypotheses and their evidence requirements.
2. **Cells and information.** Explain structures and basic inheritance using public explanatory figures.
3. **Energy and organization.** Trace energy through a conceptual organism or ecosystem diagram.
4. **Inheritance, variation and adaptation.** Identify what a toy allele-frequency graph does and does not support.
5. **Ecological interactions.** Audit a public biodiversity trend for sampling and alternative explanations.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
