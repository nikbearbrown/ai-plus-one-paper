# Branding and Marketing (AI Plus One)

**Order 50.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Position and operationalize a brand before asking AI to generate campaign material.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Marketing practitioners who know campaign basics but have not formalized brand decisions.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Audience and market evidence.** Output: Build a segment and competitor evidence grid. Check: Distinguish observed behavior from inferred motivation.
2. **Positioning and promise.** Output: Write a testable value promise and three reasons to believe. Check: Check differentiation against a direct alternative.
3. **Campaign system.** Output: Map proposition, message, channel and creative constraints across a campaign. Check: Reject a sample asset that violates the brand promise.
4. **Measurement and stewardship.** Output: Define an experiment, success threshold and claims-review process. Check: Separate attention, intent and conversion metrics.

## Assessed deliverable

A launch dossier with an independent strategy brief, two channel executions and a pre-registered measurement plan; assess message consistency and evidence. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
