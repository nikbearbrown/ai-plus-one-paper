## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Public data and provenance.** Explain what a deidentified aggregate table can and cannot support.
2. **Cohorts and visual denominators.** Reconstruct a classroom chart from synthetic counts and diagnose confounding.
3. **Measurement and missingness.** Compare sensitivity of a summary to two documented missing-data assumptions.
4. **Reproducible analysis and model audit.** Re-run a toy nonclinical figure and catch one plausible AI transcription error.
5. **Communicating research boundaries.** Produce an interpretation with confidence limits and nonclinical-use notes.

## Assessed deliverable

Reproduce a safe aggregate figure, supply a data provenance/error ledger, and stress-test an assistant output against an independent result; grade reproducibility and limit statements.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
