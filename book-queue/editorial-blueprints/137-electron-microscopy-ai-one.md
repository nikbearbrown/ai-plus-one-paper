## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Electron images and signal.** Compare illustrative pathways and identify what detector signals represent.
2. **Scale, resolution and contrast.** Calculate dimensions from a scale bar and report uncertainty.
3. **SEM and TEM perspectives.** Explain why surface and transmission images show different aspects of a harmless object.
4. **Artefacts and evidence quality.** Spot pattern differences in supplied synthetic images and supply alternative explanations.
5. **Reporting images responsibly.** Produce an annotated figure receipt with citation, scale and interpretation limits.

## Assessed deliverable

Create a comparison of safely sourced public SEM and TEM images with traceable scale and limitations; score artifact identification and comparison quality.
