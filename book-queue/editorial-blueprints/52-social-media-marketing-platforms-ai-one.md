# Social Media Marketing Platforms (AI Plus One)

**Order 52.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Make a campaign portable without pretending platform formats and reporting are interchangeable.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Campaign staff who already know audience segmentation and campaign measurement.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/social-media-marketing-platform-edition` as primary and `nikbearbrown/social-media-marketing-plus-one` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Platform affordances.** Output: Write three channel briefs for the same goal. Check: Identify where audience context demands a material change.
2. **Native creative adaptations.** Output: Prototype variants for each channel from a common asset package. Check: Check dimensions, accessibility and consistent claims.
3. **Analytics translation.** Output: Produce a platform-to-common-metric dictionary. Check: Spot two measures whose labels are misleadingly similar.
4. **Portability under change.** Output: Write an exit plan for a tool or feature deprecation. Check: Apply it to a synthetic breaking change.
