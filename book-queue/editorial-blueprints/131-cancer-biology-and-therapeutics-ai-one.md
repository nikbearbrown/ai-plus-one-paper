## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Mechanisms and evidence types.** Distinguish mechanism hypotheses from evidence of outcomes.
2. **Selective effects and tradeoffs.** Annotate a conceptual map showing why benefits and limitations can coexist.
3. **Resistance as an interpretive concept.** Explain a precomputed observational trend without proposing an experiment or intervention.
4. **Evidence hierarchy and endpoints.** Contrast abstract endpoint definitions in fictional studies.
5. **Responsible synthesis.** Write an educational comparison with independently verified sources and no recommendations.

## Assessed deliverable

Build a mechanism-to-evidence map comparing broad therapeutic classes in reviewed public literature; grade plausibility versus documented evidence, limitations and source provenance.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
