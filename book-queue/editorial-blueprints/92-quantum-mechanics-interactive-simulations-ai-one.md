## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **States and probabilities.** Sample a normalized finite state and compare measured frequencies to theory.
2. **Confinement and energy.** Vary a well width and test energy scaling.
3. **Evolution and interference.** Animate a superposition; independently compute one phase relationship.
4. **Measurement in incompatible bases.** Compare simulated basis choices and verify post-measurement state.
5. **Tunneling and discretization.** Sweep a toy barrier and distinguish a numerical boundary artifact from a physical trend.
