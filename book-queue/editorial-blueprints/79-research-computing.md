# Research Computing

**Workshop · 60 min · Queue 79 · Private Blueprint · compare**

**Audience and prerequisites.** Early graduate researchers; basic shell familiarity and a laptop or instructor-provided printout.

**Timed exercise.** 0–10 diagram local, login and compute roles; 10–25 estimate resources of a toy file-counting job; 25–40 interpret a simulated queue receipt and diagnose one deliberate resource mismatch; 40–60 verify outputs from a tiny fixed synthetic fixture and write a handoff.

**Checked take-home artifact.** Resource request and reproduction card with expected line count and checksum for the instructor-provided artifact. Compare the request to the simulated receipt and explain the discrepancy.

**Scope and caution.** No cluster login, credentials or institutional configuration needed; do not imply toy estimates or queue behavior generalize.

**Source basis.** Queue scope and indexed leads, not a claim of full source review: nikbearbrown/hpc-aicr. Related: None indexed. This is original planning, not a syllabus, approved adaptation or launch commitment.
