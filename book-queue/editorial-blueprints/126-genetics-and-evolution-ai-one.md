## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Inheritance patterns and uncertainty.** Compute a toy trait probability and state its assumptions.
2. **Variation and population models.** Compare drift and selection in an abstract precomputed model.
3. **Evolutionary evidence.** Interpret a small supplied trait-and-tree diagram with competing explanations.
4. **Adaptation and constraints.** Evaluate whether a public ecological correlation supports adaptation.
5. **Communicating what the evidence supports.** Audit a genetics headline and distinguish association from cause.
