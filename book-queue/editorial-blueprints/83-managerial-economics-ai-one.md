# Managerial Economics (AI Plus One)

**Order 83.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Translate economic models into concrete choices about pricing, capacity and strategy under uncertainty.

**Audience and prerequisites:** MBA or early-career management students with basic algebra, spreadsheets and introductory economics.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/mba-economics` as primary and `nikbearbrown/principles-economics-bundle; nikbearbrown/claude-for-economics` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Demand and elasticity.** Output: Estimate a demand response from fictional scenarios. Check: Identify sensitivity to a change in elasticities.
2. **Costs and scale.** Output: Construct marginal and average cost comparisons. Check: Distinguish sunk costs from forward-looking costs.
3. **Competition and pricing.** Output: Compare pricing under two market structures. Check: Show a rival response that changes the decision.
4. **Risk and organizational decisions.** Output: Model a staged capacity choice or incentive problem. Check: Use a sensitivity table to explain when the recommendation reverses.

## Assessed deliverable

A managerial decision memo with reproducible calculations, assumptions and a recommendation under at least two scenarios. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.

## Drafting gate

Verify MBA text against Principles and check data/figure rights. Keep management decisions as the defining graduate deliverable.
