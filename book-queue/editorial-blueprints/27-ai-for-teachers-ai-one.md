# AI for Teachers (AI Plus One)

## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** School and college instructors with basic document/LMS literacy; access to AI tools optional.

**Teaching spine:** Design an AI-supported activity that is assessable, permissioned and no less accessible without a particular tool.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Choose what students must learn unaided.** Translate one broad objective into an observable task; mark actions that may and may not be delegated.
2. **Make AI assistance transparent.** Use a synthetic learner record to design disclosure and protect identifiers; compare a no-tool route.
3. **Check generated instructional content.** Audit a classroom handout against given sources; replace one unsupported statement.
4. **Assess process and independent work.** Construct an anchored rubric that rewards decision rationale and held-out transfer, not elapsed time or stylistic guesses.
5. **Review before class adoption.** Use dummy student/teacher roles or a local simulation to test access, keyboard usability and a recovery alternative.

## Assessed deliverable

Submit a ready-to-review lesson packet: outcome, first attempt, allowed assistance, evidence and rubric, accessible no-tool alternative, privacy/access checklist. Grade designed evidence, not production volume.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.

## Drafting gate

Confirm author material and current local permissions; do not load protected student records or claim certification, learning improvement or local deployment from a tool test.
