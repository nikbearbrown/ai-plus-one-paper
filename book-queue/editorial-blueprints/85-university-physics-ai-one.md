# University Physics (AI Plus One)

**Order 85.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Connect the major domains of calculus-based introductory physics through models, derivations and reproducible predictions.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Engineering and science students taking or having taken differential and integral calculus.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/university-physics-bundle-with-llms` as primary and `nikbearbrown/physics-with-llms; Medhavy/physics-vol-1` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Mechanics and conservation.** Output: Model motion and energy transfer using free-body diagrams and integrals. Check: Check an energy prediction by an independent force calculation.
2. **Waves and thermodynamics.** Output: Construct a coupled example of oscillation or heat flow. Check: Identify which boundary conditions control the result.
3. **Fields and circuits.** Output: Use field laws and simple circuit models to predict measured quantities. Check: Reconcile signs and units through a second representation.
4. **Light and the modern transition.** Output: Compare a classical wave prediction and one quantum limit. Check: State where the introductory model no longer applies.

## Assessed deliverable

A cross-domain portfolio of original worked problems and a small measurement/simulation with uncertainty. Grade equations, checks and interpretation. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
