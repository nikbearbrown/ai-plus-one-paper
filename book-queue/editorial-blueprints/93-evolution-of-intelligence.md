# Evolution of Intelligence

**Popular nonfiction · Editorial blueprint for author review**

## Reader

Curious readers of natural history and evolution; no biology prerequisites.

## Thesis and angle

Cognition evolved as a set of solutions to recurring ecological problems, not as a single ladder toward humans. Ask of each capacity why it might have persisted, what it cost, and what evidence would distinguish rival explanations.

## Source basis

[Primary outline](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/biology-evolution-of-intelligence/blob/main/outline.md) is a template and retains older branding. The primary repo’s named chapter sequence overlaps Intelligence in definition, steering, learning, social cognition, collective intelligence, and extended mind. Its simulation material and existing chapter bodies must be compared before approving new text. The evolutionary angle below is a proposed distinction, not a verified distinct manuscript.

## Chapter arcs

1. **Why spend energy on information?** Set the ecological trade-off with a foraging problem.
2. **What learning buys:** contrast stable and changing environments and the costs of flexibility.
3. **Different bodies, different solutions:** compare lineages and distinguish convergence and ancestry.
4. **The minds of groups:** test which social or collective behaviors warrant stronger cognition claims.
5. **Culture changes the game:** examine cumulative transmission while separating biological and cultural mechanisms.
6. **No finish line:** return to the same ecological puzzle in several lineages and show why there is no single winner.

## Evidence gates

Check behavioral and phylogenetic claims against primary studies; make ancestral-state inference and uncertainty visible. Have an evolutionary biology specialist review adaptive claims and plausible alternatives. Verify authorship and where variants overlap.

## Catalog fit

Use shared animal cases with Intelligence only when they answer a new “how did this evolve?” question. No duplicate chapter or rebranding.
