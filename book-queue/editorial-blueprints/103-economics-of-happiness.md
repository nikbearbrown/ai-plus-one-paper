# Economics of Happiness

**Workshop · 45 min · Queue 103 · Private Blueprint · hold**

**Audience and prerequisites.** Introductory economics learners; percentage and graph literacy.

**Timed exercise.** 0–10 define and compare two fictional life-satisfaction indicators; 10–22 calculate changes from a synthetic table; 22–35 compare competing explanations for the same cross-sectional pattern; 35–45 revise a causal headline to match the data.

**Checked take-home artifact.** One checked table and a short memo that separates description from inference. Verify computations against a key and have a peer identify one omitted variable.

**Scope and caution.** Private lead only; no claimed manuscript reading or reuse. Synthetic data, no clinical or individual welfare judgments.
