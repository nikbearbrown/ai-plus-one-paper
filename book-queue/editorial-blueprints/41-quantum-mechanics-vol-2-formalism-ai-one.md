## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Readers familiar with one-dimensional wavefunctions, linear algebra and multivariable calculus.

**Teaching spine:** Use operator formalism, symmetries and angular momentum to explain the states and observables of the three-dimensional atom.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Operators and spectral measurement.** Compute an operator's effect in two bases and check eigenvalues, normalization and Hermiticity in a finite example.
2. **Translate symmetry into quantum numbers.** Derive simple commutators and use a raising/lowering calculation to test an allowed state.
3. **Separate radial and angular motion.** Transform a central-potential equation and check angular boundary conditions and dimensions.
4. **Hydrogen and degeneracy.** Connect quantum numbers to an energy-level chart and check counts under explicitly stated assumptions.
5. **Add angular momentum.** Check a small coupling example with a dimension count and compare the meaning of the two bases.

## Assessed deliverable

A derivation dossier from a central-potential setup to one hydrogen result: labeled bases, units, boundary conditions, commutators and a known limiting case. Grade a separately checked derivation, not software output.
