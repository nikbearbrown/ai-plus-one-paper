# Research Writing

Private workshop Blueprint • `research-writing-ai-one` • 75 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Early researchers with one question and no need for real participant data.

**Case:** A fictional lab note and a small synthetic result table.

## Run of show

- 0–15: Write one claim and what evidence would falsify it, before asking for text.
- 15–35: Use AI or a mock response to propose a paragraph; mark evidence and inference separately.
- 35–60: Verify values, remove an unsupported conclusion and draft a limitation.
- 60–75: Peer reconstructs the claim from the evidence without reading the prose first.

## Artifact and verification

One claim-evidence paragraph, figure caption and review memo; check a cited result and disclose why one inference was withheld.

**Boundary:** A writing methods exercise, not a publication claim. No invented citations, observations or human subjects.
