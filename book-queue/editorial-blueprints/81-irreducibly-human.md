# Irreducibly Human

**Popular nonfiction · Editorial blueprint for author review**

## Reader

Readers navigating delegation at work, in education, or in daily decisions.

## Thesis and angle

The question is not what humans do that machines cannot imitate, but which judgments and responsibilities cannot be handed off without changing what the work means. Follow recurring decisions where speed, competence, and accountability diverge.

## Source basis

[Primary outline](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/irreducibly-human-book/blob/main/outline.md) is a template. The primary repository has seven substantive chapter slots under historical tier names, plus introduction and conclusion; these are inventory findings, not chapter-body validation. Reconcile that material with the current author term **facets** and compare the root and volume editions before reuse.

## Chapter arcs

1. **Who receives the result?** Open with a plausible machine recommendation and name who bears the cost if it is wrong.
2. **Patterns, hands, and people:** distinguish recognized patterns from embodied experience and social interpretation.
3. **When to stop and ask why:** use supervisory, causal, and counterfactual judgment to probe apparent success.
4. **What groups know:** follow how responsibility and knowledge move across a team, rather than residing in a single operator.
5. **What cannot be commissioned:** explore purpose, wisdom, and accountability; return to the opening choice with a practical delegation boundary.

## Evidence gates

Distinguish facets as an author framework, empirical observations, and normative claims. Verify permission and provenance for stories. Any occupational concentration claim must name the actual O*NET Abilities scope; do not generalize to all of O*NET or treat unpublished work as validated.

## Catalog fit

Not a claim of permanent uniquely human capability. Distinct from Intelligence as taxonomy/measurement and from Computational Skepticism as verification of claims.
