# Figma Design with AI

Private workshop Blueprint • `figma-design-ai-one` • 75 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Designers with basic Figma navigation; use a copy or supplied mock file.

**Case:** A workshop registration card requiring normal, full and error states.

## Run of show

- 0–10: Sketch a task and success condition before using AI.
- 10–35: Make an editable component; use AI to suggest a variant, or use a supplied draft.
- 35–60: Constrain the design to shared components, check long text, contrast and prototype navigation.
- 60–75: Peer attempts registration and identifies the strongest remaining failure.

## Artifact and verification

Editable component with three states and a check sheet; verify text can change without replacing the design and errors are not color-only.

**Boundary:** No personal files or unrestricted write permissions. Figma access and optional AI automation must be verified separately.
