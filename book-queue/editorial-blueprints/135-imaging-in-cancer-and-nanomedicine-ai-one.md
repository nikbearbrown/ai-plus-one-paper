## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Signal and image formation.** Explain how a contrast pattern can vary without a structural change.
2. **Spatial sampling, scale and resolution.** Audit scale bars and a synthetic two-feature separation plot.
3. **Imaging modalities as conceptual tradeoffs.** Compare two educational modality diagrams using matched criteria.
4. **Artefacts and uncertainty.** Label ambiguity from blur or reconstruction in synthetic data.
5. **Cancer and nanomedicine image claims.** Critique published figure inferences without diagnosing or directing experiments.
