## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Historical evidence and chronology.** Critique two accounts of the same event and construct a qualified timeline.
2. **Early states and exchange.** Compare evidence from two fictionalized classroom trade-route maps grounded in reviewed history.
3. **Networks, migration and empire.** Explain a commodity's path across borders and identify whose records are absent.
4. **Industrialization and political change.** Compare regional evidence with same-scale units and independent date checks.
5. **Empire, decolonization and global interdependence.** Interpret a public archive excerpt within a historiographic dispute.

## Assessed deliverable

Create a comparative historical exhibit with two regions, an evidence dossier and a fair rival explanation; grade contextualization, source provenance and distinction between inference and fact.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
