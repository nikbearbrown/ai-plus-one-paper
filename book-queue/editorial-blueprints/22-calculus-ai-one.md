## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Students with strong algebra, functions and trigonometry; graphing and notation bridge included.

**Teaching spine:** Use limits, derivatives and integrals to move between local change, accumulated quantities and independently checked models.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Describe local behavior with limits.** Compare left/right limits and an actual sampled value in a discontinuous example.
2. **Derive rates of change.** Compute derivatives from first principles for a small function; check sign and units on a defined interval.
3. **Find and classify extrema.** Apply derivative tests and compare endpoint values; falsify an incorrect interior-only recommendation.
4. **Accumulate a changing rate.** Approximate an integral by sums and check error against a simple exact case.
5. **Connect change and accumulation.** Apply the fundamental theorem to a flow example and compare numerical, graphical and symbolic results.

## Assessed deliverable

Model fluid level in a fictional tank with a supplied flow curve. Grade units, mathematical justification, derivative/integral consistency and an error estimate for a numerical approximation.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
