## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** MBA learners with basic accounting and spreadsheet literacy.

**Teaching spine:** Decide whether a fictional firm should expand, defer, or cancel an investment after considering cash flows, financing and uncertainty.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **From earnings to project cash.** Build an incremental after-tax cash timeline; test whether including an irrelevant sunk cost changes a decision.
2. **Compare projects over time.** Compute NPV and compare alternative lives; hand-check a one-period example and explain why IRR can disagree.
3. **Estimate financing costs transparently.** Make a capital-cost assumption table; reconcile component units and market-weight totals.
4. **Choose how to finance.** Compare illustrative equity and debt paths; trace changes to cash obligations and owners’ exposures.
5. **Decide under uncertainty.** Build a break-even and scenario table; cite the largest unresolved assumption rather than presenting a single forecast as a fact.

## Assessed deliverable

Investment committee memo and reproducible model. Grade correct incremental cash flows, reconciliation of funding and investment decisions, and one sensitivity showing where the recommendation reverses.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
