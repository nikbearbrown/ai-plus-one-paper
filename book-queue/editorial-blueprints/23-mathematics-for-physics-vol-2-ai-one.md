## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Physics or applied mathematics learners who already know calculus, linear algebra and basic differential equations.

**Teaching spine:** Select and justify an advanced mathematical tool by boundary conditions, symmetry and a numerical or analytic cross-check.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Symmetry and coordinates.** Reduce a toy equation under a known symmetry; verify transformations and dimensions.
2. **Eigenfunctions and boundary conditions.** Construct a low-order mode expansion and check the boundary values and orthogonality.
3. **Spectral transforms.** Compare a transform representation with direct integration on a solvable fixture; test normalization.
4. **Perturbations and asymptotics.** Estimate a correction with its size parameter; demonstrate a regime where it stops being reliable.
5. **Computational cross-checks.** Make a convergence table for a discretized equation and compare a limiting case to the analytic answer.

## Assessed deliverable

Serve as an analyst for an idealized vibrating membrane: formulate the boundary-value model, solve a reduced case, compute a check and explain where the idealization fails.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
