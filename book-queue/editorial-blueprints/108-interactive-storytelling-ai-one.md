## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Choice, stakes and agency.** Compare two branching maps and identify a merely cosmetic choice.
2. **State-aware story structure.** Implement inventory and trust variables in a miniature dialogue.
3. **Branch convergence and pacing.** Build a reusable scene whose wording respects earlier choices.
4. **Playtest and verify.** Detect unreachable endings and unintended loops with a path checklist.
5. **AI-assisted variants without loss of voice.** Independently write the reference scene; test one generated variant for continuity and attribution.

## Assessed deliverable

Ship and test a short branching prototype with meaningful choice, comparable paths and at least one designed consequence; score state coverage, coherence, accessibility and credited sources.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
