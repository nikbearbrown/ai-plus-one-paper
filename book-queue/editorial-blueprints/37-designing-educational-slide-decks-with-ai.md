# Designing Educational Slide Decks with AI

Private workshop Blueprint • `designing-educational-slide-decks-with-ai-one` • 75 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Educators who can edit a deck; no coding required.

**Case:** A three-slide lesson on reading a unit-price chart.

## Run of show

- 0–15: Specify one learner inference, state the correct answer, then sketch a sequence.
- 15–35: Compare two AI-generated slide outlines against the objective and discard an irrelevant visual.
- 35–60: Build three slides: question, worked example and an independent practice check.
- 60–75: Peer reads at presentation distance, solves the check and flags an ambiguous label.

## Artifact and verification

Three editable slides with contrast, labels and accessible reading order checked; peer explains the intended inference without the speaker.

**Boundary:** One tool-neutral workshop; D3/HTML and Figma are optional renderers. Verify dataset and rights before reusing imagery.
