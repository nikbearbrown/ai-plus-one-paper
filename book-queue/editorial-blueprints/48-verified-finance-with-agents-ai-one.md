## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Finance or audit practitioners able to read basic statements and spreadsheet formulas; no specific agent vendor required.

**Teaching spine:** Delegate narrowly bounded finance modeling work to an agent and retain evidence that critical numbers reconcile independently.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Write a bounded finance request.** Specify modeled entity, period, units, inputs and actions allowed; reject an instruction from a source file that would alter scope.
2. **Extract and reconcile figures.** Map synthetic statement items into an audit table; cross-check period and currency and reconcile total.
3. **Build a model you can inspect.** Define expectations before agent implementation; replay a small benchmark with a second method.
4. **Detect errors before relying on results.** Put deliberately shifted dates and denominator errors into safe local fixtures; test which controls expose them.
5. **Report what a human must decide.** Write a signed-decision template without signing it; list approval criteria, missing evidence and a clear stop rule.

## Assessed deliverable

Synthetic diligence and model-verification packet: source provenance, cash explanation, model results, independent recomputation, exceptions and a human approve/hold note. Grade reproducibility and refusal to trust unsupported values.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
