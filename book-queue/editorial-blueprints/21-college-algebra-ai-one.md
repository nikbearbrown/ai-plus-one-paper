## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Learners fluent with fractions and one-variable equations; suitable before precalculus or applied modeling.

**Teaching spine:** Move fluently among algebraic forms, graphs and contexts and check where a symbolic manipulation changes the allowed solutions.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Treat functions as mappings.** Compare tables, formulas and graphs and state domains of composed functions.
2. **Solve polynomial and rational equations.** Track legal operations and detect an extraneous solution through substitution.
3. **Use exponential and logarithmic models.** Fit a small growth example and verify inverse operations and units.
4. **Solve systems and inequalities.** Graph a feasible region and verify candidate points against original constraints.
5. **Build and critique a model.** Compare two reasonable functions against a held-out point and a specified extrapolation failure.

## Assessed deliverable

Build and defend a piecewise demand model from invented observations. Grade domain, algebra, graph and an independent challenge case; include a solution rejected by a domain restriction.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.

## Drafting gate

Confirm numbering/source credit and compare variants. Audit domain and extraneous-root examples with substitution; keep routine prealgebra remediation in a bridge.
