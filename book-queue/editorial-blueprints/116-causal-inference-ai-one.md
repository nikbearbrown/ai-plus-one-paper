## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Counterfactuals and estimands.** Define a population, treatment, outcome and contrast for a toy tutoring policy.
2. **Randomization and imperfect compliance.** Compare intent-to-treat and selected subgroup estimates.
3. **Causal diagrams and adjustment.** Identify a confounder and explain why conditioning on a collider fails.
4. **Quasi-experiments and diagnostics.** Test a synthetic pretrend or cutoff assumption; flag one failure.
5. **Sensitivity and reporting.** Compare the effect under altered assumptions and document where conclusions stop.

## Assessed deliverable

Deliver an original synthetic or licensed-public case with estimand, design rationale, balance diagnostics and sensitivity analysis; score independent reproduction and explicit nonidentification where warranted.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
