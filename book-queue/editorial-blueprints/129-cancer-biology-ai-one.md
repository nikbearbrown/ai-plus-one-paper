## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Normal organization and loss of control.** Map conceptual differences between a tissue schematic and a neoplastic model.
2. **Somatic variation and growth.** Interpret a toy population graph and distinguish drivers from associated observations.
3. **The tumor and its environment.** Compare hypotheses for a diagram depicting interacting cell types.
4. **Evolution and heterogeneity.** Track abstract simulated populations and state what figures cannot establish.
5. **Evidence across scales.** Critique a published figure's scope without attempting new experiments.

## Assessed deliverable

Build a mechanism map and evidence critique using fictional diagrams and safe public figures; score scale, conceptual accuracy, uncertainty and attribution.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
