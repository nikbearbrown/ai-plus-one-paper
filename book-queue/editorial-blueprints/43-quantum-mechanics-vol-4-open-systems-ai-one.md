## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Readers with quantum state vectors, matrix algebra and basic probability; optional introductory computation.

**Teaching spine:** Represent uncertainty, correlations and dynamics in small quantum systems and verify that a model preserves physical constraints.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **From pure states to density operators.** Construct a mixture and verify trace and positivity; distinguish it from a coherent superposition by a measured observable.
2. **Compare joint and reduced states.** Compute a partial trace and inspect a correlation indicator without inferring entanglement from correlation alone.
3. **Describe measurement operationally.** Compute outcome probabilities and post-measurement states for one small example and check totals.
4. **Model an open system.** Build a toy decay or dephasing channel; verify completeness and one known fixed-point behavior.
5. **Interpret information from outcomes.** Design a small simulated discrimination task and explain which conclusions require more experiments.

## Assessed deliverable

Analyze a two-qubit synthetic noise experiment: states, reduced state, measurement, channel check and uncertainty. Grade trace/positivity checks and distinction between model prediction and observation.
