## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** MBA or advanced undergraduate readers familiar with business fundamentals who want to analyze incomplete cases without a prescribed answer.

**Teaching spine:** Distill messy evidence into a decision, compare truly different options, and defend a recommendation under challenge.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Read for the decision.** Write who must decide what and when; separate disclosed facts from narrative cues.
2. **Diagnose the bottleneck.** Create a causal hypothesis and financial or operating sanity check; challenge with a counterexample.
3. **Develop alternatives with tradeoffs.** Compare action, staged trial and do-nothing option; state a shared criterion.
4. **Ask for missing evidence.** Draft questions and test which answer could reverse the decision; keep assumptions separate from evidence.
5. **Write and defend the recommendation.** Supply a memo plus an observable implementation check, then update when a conflicting fact arrives.

## Assessed deliverable

A 2-page recommendation, evidence table and live defense for a wholly fictional service expansion. Grade alternatives, numerical check, dissent, implementation and response to an unseen fact.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
