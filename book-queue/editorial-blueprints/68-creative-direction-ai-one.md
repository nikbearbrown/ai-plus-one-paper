# Creative Direction (AI Plus One)

**Order 68.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Make creative judgment explicit enough to direct an AI-assisted studio without surrendering art direction.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Noncoding creative directors or small-studio leads who can articulate audience and brand context.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/agentic-creative-direction` as primary and `nikbearbrown/branding-and-ai; nikbearbrown/the-creative-engineer; nikbearbrown/brutalist-figma-claude; nikbearbrown/figma-webdesigner` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **From brief to criteria.** Output: Write a creative brief, visual references and rejection tests. Check: Explain what aesthetic tradeoff requires a director.
2. **Lead divergent explorations.** Output: Design three contrasting concepts and a fair review process. Check: Defend selection without over-weighting polish.
3. **Coordinate production.** Output: Set asset decisions, review rounds and accountability. Check: Recover from a simulated approval or continuity failure.
4. **Deliver an auditable campaign.** Output: Curate final outputs and a provenance/release checklist. Check: Trace every published claim to evidence.

## Assessed deliverable

A mock campaign kit with director’s brief, comparison board and accountable approval record. Grade clarity of judgment and consistency across assets. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.

Compare actual branding syllabus and permissions. Retain only if studio roles, audience and judgment artifacts differ materially from #50/67.
