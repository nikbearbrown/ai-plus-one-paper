## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Kinematics and uncertainty.** Fit displacement data to a constant-acceleration model and inspect residuals.
2. **Forces and momentum.** Draw free-body diagrams and compare two collision approximations.
3. **Work, energy and rotation.** Predict a rolling body's energy partition and defend the assumptions.
4. **Oscillations and damping.** Estimate a natural period from synthetic data and test a damping trend.
5. **Wave propagation and superposition.** Predict standing-wave modes and verify a simulation with scale and units.

## Assessed deliverable

Submit a mechanics-to-waves laboratory dossier with predictions, units and uncertainty using a benign simulation or public dataset; grade reproducibility and discrepant findings.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
