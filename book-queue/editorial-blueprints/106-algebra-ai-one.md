## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Expressions and equivalence.** Simplify two forms and test equality on allowed inputs.
2. **Linear equations and systems.** Model two overlapping pricing rules and check an intersection.
3. **Functions, inverses and domains.** Test whether inputs can be recovered and where inverse rules fail.
4. **Polynomial and rational relationships.** Compare roots, factors, asymptotes and excluded values in a graph.
5. **Modeling with multiple representations.** Explain an equation-to-graph-to-table translation and check assumptions.
