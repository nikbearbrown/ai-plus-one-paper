## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **What counts as evidence of relatedness.** Interpret a supplied character matrix and justify alternative trees.
2. **Microbial and multicellular diversity.** Compare public life-history diagrams without using laboratory procedures.
3. **Plants, fungi and animals.** Build a trait matrix and identify convergent traits rather than mere visual similarity.
4. **Species in place.** Analyze public occurrence counts and flag uneven observer effort.
5. **Conservation and ecosystem roles.** Evaluate a synthetic habitat scenario using multiple indicators.
