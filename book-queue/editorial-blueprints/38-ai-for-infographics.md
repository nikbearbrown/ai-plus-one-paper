# AI for Infographics

Private workshop Blueprint • `ai-for-infographics-ai-one` • 60 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Educators or communicators who can read a table and annotate a figure.

**Case:** A synthetic table of three library branches with counts and unequal totals.

## Run of show

- 0–10: Calculate the relevant percentages by hand and decide what comparison is fair.
- 10–25: Generate or inspect a candidate infographic and audit its denominator and visual encoding.
- 25–45: Correct labels and scale; provide meaningful alt text and a source note.
- 45–60: Swap figures and recompute one value directly from the table.

## Artifact and verification

One editable infographic with an accessible text alternative and a checked numerator/denominator pair.

**Boundary:** No unsupported causal claims, generated data or inferred results. Distinct from dashboard and D3 implementation workshops.
