## Purpose and reader

## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Microscopic life and classification.** Compare reviewed cell diagrams and explain the distinction between observation and category.
2. **Microbial roles in environments.** Trace an abstract nutrient-cycle diagram and compare two benign ecosystems.
3. **Community observations and sampling limits.** Interpret aggregate public measurements without giving sampling protocols.
4. **Ordinary uses and ecological tradeoffs.** Compare food or wastewater examples descriptively, with no process instructions.
5. **Claims and microbial evidence.** Audit a news claim and distinguish presence from demonstrated ecological effect.

## Assessed deliverable

Create a public-evidence exhibit comparing microbial roles in three benign settings; grade taxonomy, diagram accuracy, evidence limitations and context.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
