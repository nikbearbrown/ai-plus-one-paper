# Classical Mechanics (AI Plus One)

**Order 86.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Teach students to choose a mechanical formulation and show how symmetry and constraints shape motion.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Students who know introductory calculus-based mechanics and ordinary differential equations.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/physics-classical-mechanics` as primary and `nikbearbrown/physics-plus-one-classical-mechanics; nikbearbrown/claude-for-physics` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Coordinates and constraints.** Output: Express a pendulum with an appropriate generalized coordinate. Check: Check that the constraint does not add a spurious degree of freedom.
2. **Energy and variational mechanics.** Output: Derive an equation using a Lagrangian and compare it with force reasoning. Check: Check a limiting case.
3. **Central forces and coupled oscillations.** Output: Analyze orbits or small vibrations in a simple conservative model. Check: Confirm that the conserved quantity remains constant numerically.
4. **Hamiltonian mechanics.** Output: Convert a stated system to phase-space form and interpret a trajectory. Check: Check numerical drift against the expected invariant.

## Assessed deliverable

A dynamics dossier with two independent derivations, a bounded numerical trajectory and an explanation of errors and constraints. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
