## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Scarcity, gains from trade and opportunity cost.** Draw a production frontier and test a comparative advantage claim.
2. **Markets and elasticity.** Predict a market response and calculate burden under alternative elasticities.
3. **Market structure and externalities.** Compare stylized competitive and concentrated outcomes using a counterexample.
4. **Output, inflation and employment.** Interpret public macro indicators after checking units and reference years.
5. **Fiscal and monetary responses.** Compare two simplified policy scenarios and flag assumptions that could reverse the result.
