# Validating AI Output (AI Plus One)

**Order 80.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Make an everyday validity check repeatable: identify the claim, seek independent evidence, record residual uncertainty, and decide whether to use it.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Non-specialist practitioners who use AI-generated drafts, summaries and simple analyses, not evaluation engineers.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/validating-output-from-ai-systems` as primary and `nikbearbrown/computational-skepticism-for-ai; nikbearbrown/critique` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Spot the verifiable claim.** Output: Turn a fluent summary into checkable propositions. Check: Separate stated facts from implied conclusions.
2. **Trace the source.** Output: Compare statements to original documents and tables. Check: Catch one unsupported quote or denominator.
3. **Check artifacts.** Output: Recompute a reported total and run a small code check on a toy fixture. Check: Find where a plausible output diverges from expected results.
4. **Decide and document.** Output: Choose accept, revise, escalate or reject for each claim. Check: Write a usable limitations note.

## Assessed deliverable

A short evidence ledger covering a narrative, table and safe computational output; measure accurate triage and quality of supporting evidence. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
