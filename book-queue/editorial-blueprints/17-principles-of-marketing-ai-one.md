## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Beginning marketing learners with basic numeracy and no platform training.

**Teaching spine:** Build an ethical go-to-market decision from evidence of who needs a product, what makes it different and how to test delivery.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Find the customer's problem.** Build a research-question and segment table; identify an unsupported generalization from a small sample.
2. **Specify a promise and competitor.** Write a positioning statement and evidence ledger; compare against a substitute, not just one rival.
3. **Price and distribute coherently.** Show how cost, buyer effort and price constrain adoption; sanity-check one unit-economics example.
4. **Choose communication channels.** Sketch a channel experiment with accessibility and permission checks; separate reach from conversion.
5. **Measure and revise the mix.** Compute a funnel and retention signals from synthetic data; treat a baseline difference as descriptive until design supports an effect.

## Assessed deliverable

A marketing plan for a fictional tool library with positioning, mix, an ethical field-test simulation and a measurement dashboard. Grade alignment, baseline and explicit unknowns, not design polish.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
