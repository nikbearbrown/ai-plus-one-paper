## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Introduction for business readers who know basic arithmetic but have not closed an accounting cycle.

**Teaching spine:** Transform business events into reconciled statements and explain the judgment hidden behind accounting entries.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Trace a transaction twice.** Create a ledger and trial balance; reconcile entries for three transactions without using generated balances as checks.
2. **Recognize timing.** Compare prepaid expense, accounts receivable and cash recognition; show the balance and income impacts at period end.
3. **Measure inventory and assets.** Compute selected inventory costs and depreciation from a fixed schedule; reconcile against units and carrying amounts.
4. **Read the financial statements together.** Prepare income, balance and cash-flow views; trace why net income does not equal cash movement.
5. **Test internal consistency.** Find a seeded classification or cutoff error; document the correction without assuming a balanced ledger proves accuracy.

## Assessed deliverable

Close a synthetic repair business month from transaction slips to the three statements. Grade balance, accrual logic, inventory/asset treatment and reconciliation; include one intentionally misposted item.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
