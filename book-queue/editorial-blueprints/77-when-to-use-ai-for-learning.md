# When to Use AI for Learning

**Workshop · 45 min · Queue 77 · Private Blueprint · compare**

**Audience and prerequisites.** College students and instructors; bring one fictional assignment brief.

**Timed exercise.** 0–8 classify independent learning, assisted production and assessment; 8–18 solve a small interpretation problem unaided and record confidence; 18–32 compare a supplied AI response with an answer key and decide whether to accept, revise or decline; 32–45 apply the decision rule to an unfamiliar case.

**Checked take-home artifact.** One-page decision log with a before/after claim and independent supporting evidence. A peer checks that the unaided attempt predates the AI output and the conclusion agrees with the supplied evidence.

**Scope and caution.** Use invented coursework. A classroom demonstration does not establish learning gains or override course policy.
