## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Undergraduates with calculus and elementary linear algebra who want a math preparation path tied specifically to quantum physics.

**Teaching spine:** Make the structures behind quantum calculations explicit and test correspondences between symbolic and numerical forms.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Linear spaces for states.** Project an example into two bases and verify inner products and norm invariance.
2. **Represent operators and resolutions.** Calculate a spectral decomposition and test it by matrix reconstruction.
3. **Treat differential equations as boundary problems.** Solve an elementary equation with actual boundary conditions and separate physical from mathematical assumptions.
4. **Use transforms and distributions.** Derive a simple Fourier pair and independently check normalization under a stated convention.
5. **Approximate discretely without hiding error.** Construct a small operator approximation and show grid or basis convergence against a known spectrum.

## Assessed deliverable

Produce an annotated toolbox for a one-dimensional quantum model: transform, basis, domain and numerical cross-check of one spectrum. Grade convention consistency and an independent reconstruction.
