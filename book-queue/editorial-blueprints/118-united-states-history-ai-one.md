## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Indigenous societies, colonization and exchange.** Compare where two vetted records disagree and document each perspective.
2. **Revolution, constitution and expansion.** Explain a change in governance using two credited public documents.
3. **Slavery, Civil War and Reconstruction.** Build a causal timeline and account for contradictory local evidence.
4. **Industrialization, migration and reform.** Compare one public demographic series with a primary narrative.
5. **Twentieth-century rights, state power and memory.** Analyze evolving claims without substituting hindsight for contemporary evidence.
