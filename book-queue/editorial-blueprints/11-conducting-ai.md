# Conducting AI

Private workshop Blueprint • `conducting-ai-one` • 60 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Professionals who can edit a document; no coding required.

**Case:** An unverified municipal event brief and four synthetic source cards.

## Run of show

- 0–10: Write the decision, constraints and an independent answer before asking a model.
- 10–25: Delegate drafting only and mark unsupported claims in the proposed brief.
- 25–45: Cross-check each material claim against the cards; revise one claim and document one omission.
- 45–60: Swap briefs and defend the decision and unresolved uncertainty.

## Artifact and verification

A decision memo and evidence ledger; peer can reproduce two claims and identify the decision the human retained.

**Boundary:** Use the course as a topic lead, not an authorization to copy assignments. This is human direction and judgment, not agent engineering.
