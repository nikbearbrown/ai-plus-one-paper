# Research Methods in AI (AI Plus One)

**Order 73.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Teach how to turn a plausible AI idea into a study whose claims can survive a direct attempt to disprove them.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Graduate students with an AI application idea and basic statistics; prior publication not assumed.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/CSYE_INFO_7374_Research_Methods_in_AI; nikbearbrown/the-research-paper-recipe` as primary and `nikbearbrown/critique; nikbearbrown/Effective-Prompting-for-Literature-Reviews` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **From idea to question.** Output: State a bounded research question and claim/evidence table. Check: Tell a feasibility result from an effectiveness result.
2. **Compare with prior work.** Output: Build a traceable literature inclusion and contrast matrix. Check: Independently verify key citations and exclusions.
3. **Design a credible comparison.** Output: Choose data split, baseline, outcomes and stopping rule. Check: Identify one source of leakage or confounding.
4. **Conduct, interpret and communicate.** Output: Write a reproducible study plan with negative-result and limits reporting. Check: Run an adversarial review of an unsupported conclusion.

## Assessed deliverable

A prospective research protocol, small synthetic pilot, reproducibility package and reviewer response. Grade claim design, evidence and transparency, not novelty alone. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
