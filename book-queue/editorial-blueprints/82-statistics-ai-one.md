# Statistics with AI (AI Plus One)

**Order 82.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Teach students to reason about data and uncertainty first, then use AI to challenge rather than replace that reasoning.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Introductory statistics learners with algebra and basic data-table skills.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/math-statistics` as primary and `nikbearbrown/introductory-statistics-bundle-with-llms; nikbearbrown/claude-for-statistics` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Sampling and description.** Output: Summarize a sample and identify its likely selection biases. Check: Compare graphical and numerical summaries for one outlier.
2. **Randomness and variability.** Output: Build a simulation of repeated sampling. Check: Compare empirical variation with a hand-calculated example.
3. **Inference.** Output: Choose and interpret an interval and a hypothesis test. Check: Reject the mistaken claim that a p-value is the probability the null is true.
4. **Regression and communication.** Output: Fit and diagnose a simple regression and report uncertainty. Check: Spot extrapolation from a misleading proposed summary.

## Assessed deliverable

A complete analysis report with hand-checkable calculations, reproduction notebook and a critique of an AI interpretation. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
