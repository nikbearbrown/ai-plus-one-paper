## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Authority, jurisdiction and case reading.** Identify which source can govern a fictional cross-state dispute.
2. **Contracts and performance.** Build an issue tree for a fictional supply dispute and document missing facts.
3. **Organizations and duties.** Compare entity choice and oversight in a fictional two-founder scenario.
4. **Business harms and compliance.** Distinguish concepts of civil liability and regulation using classroom cases.
5. **Evidence and decisions.** Rebuild a short issue-rule-application-conclusion analysis and note counsel referral points.

## Assessed deliverable

Produce a fictional business issue-spotting memo with traceable authority and competing applications; score jurisdiction/date, reasoning and when to consult counsel.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
