## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Structure, scale and orientation.** Label a schematic by anatomical planes and identify a misleading view.
2. **Movement and support.** Use a simple lever diagram to predict torque direction.
3. **Transport and exchange.** Trace oxygen along a generic circulation and gas-exchange map.
4. **Neural, endocrine and feedback control.** Explain an abstract feedback loop and identify what changes under delay.
5. **Integration across organ systems.** Model ordinary exercise using a supplied fictional evidence table.

## Assessed deliverable
