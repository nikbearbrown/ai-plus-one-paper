# AI for Writing (AI Plus One)

**Order 78.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Use AI to test writing choices and alternatives without handing it authorship or epistemic responsibility.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Working writers and students who can draft independently and want editing support.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/ai-for-writing-a-practitioners-guide` as primary and `nikbearbrown/writing-guide-with-llms; nikbearbrown/writing-guide; nikbearbrown/claude-for-writing` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Purpose, reader and claim.** Output: Write an independent brief identifying intended reader action. Check: Show why a persuasive claim needs stronger evidence.
2. **Draft and diagnose.** Output: Produce a draft and evidence-backed revision plan. Check: Compare human diagnosis with an AI critique and reject one bad suggestion.
3. **Revise voice and structure.** Output: Edit structure, pacing and tone across two genres. Check: Check the revisions preserve meaning and attribution.
4. **Verify and deliver.** Output: Create a source and permissions checklist and version record. Check: Restore the prior version if changes compromise intent.

## Assessed deliverable

A portfolio of two original pieces with first drafts, annotated revisions, source checks and an assistance disclosure. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.

## Drafting gate

Verify primary guide scope and provenance; retain separately only if practical cross-genre writing differs from the research-methods and paper-writing tracks.
