## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Time value and cash-flow timelines.** Discount two unequal payment streams and independently check one value.
2. **Risk and return.** Compare expected value and dispersion for toy outcomes without personal advice.
3. **Debt and equity claims.** Trace fictional payment priority and yield sensitivity.
4. **Valuation with assumptions.** Model a small company with alternative cash-flow forecasts and explain range.
5. **Household and business decisions as cases.** Compare liquidity and uncertainty in invented scenarios without regulated or personal advice.
