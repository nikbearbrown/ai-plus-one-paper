# Data Visualization and Infographics

Private workshop Blueprint • `data-visualization-and-infographics-ai-one` • 75 minutes • noncredit proposal.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Analysts and designers comfortable with counts, rates and denominators.

**Case:** Dashboard for a fictional bike-share service with unequal sample sizes and a missing month.

## Run of show

- 0–15: Write the decision and independently compute a rate with its denominator.
- 15–35: Compare a time-series view and a cross-station comparison; identify misleading scales or missing observations.
- 35–60: Build a summary dashboard with a chart, clear units and an uncertainty or missing-data label.
- 60–75: Give the dashboard to a peer to answer one operational question; check their answer against the table.

## Artifact and verification

A dashboard sketch or editable file, source table and check log; recompute a rate and verify missing data are not shown as zero.
