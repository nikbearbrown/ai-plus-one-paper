# Intellectual Property (AI Plus One)

**Order 84.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Teach how to recognize intellectual-property issues and document appropriate questions for professional review.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Business and design students with no prior legal study.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/mba-intellectual-property` as primary and `nikbearbrown/claude-for-intellectual-property` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Identify protected interests.** Output: Classify issues in a fictional product launch. Check: Explain why ownership and permission are separate questions.
2. **Ownership and licensing.** Output: Read illustrative license clauses and build a permissions matrix. Check: Flag agreements requiring jurisdiction-specific advice.
3. **Trademarks and disclosure.** Output: Evaluate a fictional brand and design scenario. Check: Distinguish questions about identification, confusion and public disclosure.
4. **AI and asset provenance.** Output: Document source, human contribution and reuse conditions for synthetic project assets. Check: Reject any unsupported assertion of legal ownership.

## Assessed deliverable

An educational launch issue-spotting memo and rights register for fictional materials, not legal advice or a clearance decision. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.

## Drafting gate

Compare with Order 18 Introduction to Intellectual Property (same primary source); do not open a second general book without distinct scope. Verify authoritative law, dates and jurisdiction before drafting; confirm source and illustrative-material rights.
