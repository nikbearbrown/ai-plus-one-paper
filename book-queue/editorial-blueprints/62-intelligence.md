# Intelligence

**Popular nonfiction · Editorial blueprint for author review**

## Reader

Curious readers of popular science who have wondered whether an animal or a machine is intelligent.

## Thesis and angle

Intelligence is a question before it is a score: a test reveals the capacity it was designed to elicit. Use comparable challenges, then ask what the comparison hides.

## Source basis

[Intelligence outline](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/intelligence/blob/main/outline.md) checks as a substantive existing plan: capacity tests, multiple definitions, and an Extension Note device. Preserve that architecture; these arcs are an editorial map, not replacement chapters.

## Chapter arcs

1. **What are we asking?** Put rival definitions against one challenge and show how the verdict changes.
2. **Before the brain:** begin with steering and adaptation without assuming reasoning.
3. **Learning and remembering:** compare the evidence used to distinguish an acquired response from a built-in one.
4. **Minds in different bodies:** compare animal cases where perception or the test apparatus changes apparent performance.
5. **Tools that extend us:** collect the Extension Notes and ask when a machine adds a capacity rather than displaying the same one.
6. **The next fair test:** end with improved questions, not a league table.

## Evidence gates

Check each behavioral example against the primary protocol, control, and alternative interpretation. Don’t infer consciousness from successful performance or equivalent mechanisms from similar results. Assess speculative variants separately.

## Catalog fit

Owns the comparison and measurement of capacities. Evolution of Intelligence owns origins and evolutionary explanations; Irreducibly Human owns delegation and responsibility.
