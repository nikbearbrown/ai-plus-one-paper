## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** MBA entrants who can do basic arithmetic and read a simple chart; no calculus requirement for the core.

**Teaching spine:** Translate business assumptions into sensible quantities, relationships and sensitivity calculations.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Work with units and rates.** Convert between daily and monthly demand; show how a denominator change alters meaning.
2. **Model linear relationships.** Build cost and revenue lines and calculate break-even; verify the intersection numerically.
3. **Grow quantities over time.** Contrast simple and compound growth and discuss the extrapolation limit.
4. **Use probability for planning.** Calculate expected costs for a toy demand scenario; compare expectation with downside.
5. **Optimize and communicate.** Compare two resource plans under fixed constraints and test one alternative by substitution.

## Assessed deliverable

Forecast a synthetic service team's demand, capacity, margin and financing sensitivity in one worksheet. Grade equations, unit consistency, independent benchmark and plain-language explanation.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
