# Electromagnetism (AI Plus One)

**Order 87.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Derive electromagnetic results from fields and boundaries and use independent checks on physical interpretation.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Physics or engineering students with calculus-based mechanics and vector calculus.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/physics-electromagnetism` as primary and `nikbearbrown/physics-plus-one-electromagnetism; nikbearbrown/claude-for-physics` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Charge and electrostatic fields.** Output: Solve a symmetric field problem and justify the surface or boundary choices. Check: Compare dimensions and distinguished asymptotes.
2. **Potential and material boundaries.** Output: Show how a field follows from potential in a simple geometry. Check: Check continuity conditions at an interface.
3. **Magnetic fields and induction.** Output: Compute flux and induced response in a safe mathematical example. Check: Explain predicted sign using a second representation.
4. **Maxwell equations and waves.** Output: Derive a simple wave relation and predict energy flow. Check: Check the vacuum limit and dimensional consistency.

## Assessed deliverable

A field-modeling portfolio combining original derivations, conceptual boundary checks and numerical agreement on a safe toy setup. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.
