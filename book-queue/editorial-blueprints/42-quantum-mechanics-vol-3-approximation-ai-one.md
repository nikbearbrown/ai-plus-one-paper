## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Students comfortable with ket notation, Hamiltonians and simple exact spectra.

**Teaching spine:** Choose approximation methods by regime and test whether calculated corrections describe a realistic system.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Perturb an exactly solved model.** Compute a first-order shift and compare error scaling against exact diagonalization.
2. **Handle a near-degeneracy.** Choose a restricted subspace and check the corrected splitting against original symmetries.
3. **Bound an energy with a trial state.** Construct a variational upper bound under stated conditions; check normalization and compare a second ansatz.
4. **Explain driven transitions.** Specify assumed driving and time regime; test selection-rule or resonance reasoning on a simple fixture.
5. **Test an approximation on a real model.** Use a documented idealized system; compare regimes, residuals and credible experimental interpretation without claiming a new measurement.

## Assessed deliverable

Methods comparison for a model atom plus a solvable control: perturbative, variational and small numerical calculations with assumptions, error and mismatch explanation.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
