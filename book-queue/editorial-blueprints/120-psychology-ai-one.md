## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **Methods, ethics and replication.** Classify designs in three short examples and spot one threatened inference.
2. **Sensation, perception, attention and memory.** Interpret a supplied anonymized attention graph and compare rival models.
3. **Learning, decision and motivation.** Predict a toy behavioral trend and grade the fit against synthetic observations.
4. **Development, identity and social behavior.** Compare evidence across two cohorts without generalizing beyond them.
5. **Individual variation and well-being.** Audit a public psychology headline for overclaiming and separate education from assessment.
