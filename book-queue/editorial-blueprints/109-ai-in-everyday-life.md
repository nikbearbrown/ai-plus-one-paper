# AI in Everyday Life

**Workshop · 45 min · Queue 109 · Private Blueprint · hold**

**Audience and prerequisites.** Adults new to AI; no coding and no account required.

**Timed exercise.** 0–8 choose a low-stakes task and remove personal information; 8–18 draft a make-believe event checklist unaided; 18–30 compare it to an AI sample containing a wrong date; 30–45 check the original fictional flyer and revise, showing what AI did and what stayed human.

**Checked take-home artifact.** Clean checklist and one source-backed correction. Pair-check the date, location and omitted constraint directly against the flyer.

**Scope and caution.** No real accounts, purchases, external messages, sensitive details, or high-stakes advice. Separate from advanced professional Conducting AI.
