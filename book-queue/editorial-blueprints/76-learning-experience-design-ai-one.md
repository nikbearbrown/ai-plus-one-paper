# Learning Experience Design (AI Plus One)

**Order 76.** Planning only; not authorization to draft.

## Vision

Design an accessible learning experience by linking a learner need to practice, evidence and revision.

**Audience and prerequisites:** Educators or training designers who know an audience but not formal instructional alignment.

**Source basis:** Queue lists `nikbearbrown/learning-experience-design-lxd; nikbearbrown/ai-for-learning-experience-design` as primary and `nikbearbrown/claude-for-education` as related. These are queue leads, not independently reviewed sources for this plan.

## Learning architecture and chapter checks

1. **Understand learners.** Output: Create a needs and constraints brief from a fictional setting. Check: Separate inferred need from observed barrier.
2. **Align outcomes and evidence.** Output: Map capabilities to checks and feedback. Check: Reject one activity not measuring its stated outcome.
3. **Prototype a practice sequence.** Output: Storyboard examples, practice and transfer tasks with accessibility choices. Check: Have a peer run an example without verbal repair.
4. **Evaluate and revise.** Output: Build a pilot instrument and actionable revision memo. Check: Distinguish engagement from learning evidence.

## Assessed deliverable

An accessible mini-module with learner brief, alignment matrix, practice, assessment and pilot notes. Begin independently; log any AI assistance and independently verify its claims and outputs.

## Drafting gate

Compare both actual course outlines and rights; keep AI support subordinate to learning evidence.
