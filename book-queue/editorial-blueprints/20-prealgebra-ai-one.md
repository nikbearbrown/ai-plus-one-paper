## Reader and purpose

**Audience and prerequisites:** Returning or beginning college learners who want confident number sense before algebra.

**Teaching spine:** Explain and check each step from arithmetic and proportions to simple symbolic reasoning, without relying on a calculator.

## Proposed chapter progression

1. **Build number and operation sense.** Place signed values on a line and justify operation order using equivalent examples.
2. **Move among fractions, decimal and percent.** Use a hundred grid or equivalent model; independently verify conversions.
3. **Use ratios and units.** Solve a scaled recipe and unit-price comparison; show why an additive shortcut fails.
4. **Translate a statement into an equation.** Solve a one-variable balance problem and check the result by substitution.
5. **Read a table and graph.** Compare two simple patterns and identify a misleading scale without assuming causation.

## Assessed deliverable

Unassisted household-budget puzzle with worked steps. Grade signs, operations, ratio units and error correction; then use optional AI feedback only after submission and verify it with inverse operations.

Require a first unaided attempt and a checked revision. Any AI assistance is optional unless the source-verified teaching scope specifically calls for it; assess learner judgment from a separate calculation, trace, or test.
