## Chapter sequence and checks

1. **The landscape of oncology evidence.** Classify public sources by intended audience and authority.
2. **Measurement and definitions.** Compare how abstract stage, grade and study outcome serve different concepts.
3. **Screening, diagnosis and treatment as evidence questions.** Sort fictional claims into evidence requirements rather than recommendations.
4. **Fair communication under uncertainty.** Translate publicly reviewed educational material without overstepping or hiding ambiguity.
5. **Living verification ledger.** Record dates and changes for a bounded claim and flag when specialist review is mandatory.

## Assessed deliverable

Create a fictional patient-free evidence glossary and audit two public education claims for currency, evidence level and uncertainty; grade source quality and clearly bounded conclusions.

Learners submit an independent initial reasoning trail before AI assistance, then test a specific suggestion against independent evidence and document the disposition. The assessed result is the student's evidence, not the model output.
